CyberJobs Work Capture

How work gets captured fast.

Worker Fingerprint

CyberJobs Work Capture follows the natural fingerprint of the job. The worker should not feel like they are filling out paperwork. The worker should feel like they are capturing the next piece of work and moving on.

A worker performs the work. CyberJobs captures the observation around that work with as few steps as possible.

Capture Purpose

The purpose of CyberJobs Work Capture is speed. A worker should be able to capture work without stopping the job, rewriting the same information, or searching through menus.

The system should remember the current job context whenever possible. The worker should only add the new observation.

Repeat

Repeat continues the current work context. If the worker is still on the same job, same shift, same store, same room, same route, or same assignment, CyberJobs should not ask the same questions again.

Repeat protects speed by carrying forward what is already known.

Snap

Snap captures the new observation. That observation may be a shelf photo, job photo, repair photo, progress photo, barcode scan, product view, room view, equipment view, or other work image.

Snap is the worker action. The worker points, captures, and moves forward.

Next

Next prepares the worker for the next capture. The worker should not have to rebuild the same setup after every photo or scan.

Next keeps the work moving.

What May Be Attached Automatically

CyberJobs Work Capture may attach known context around the capture so the worker does not have to enter it repeatedly.

  • Worker
  • Job
  • Assignment
  • Place
  • Time
  • Photo
  • Barcode, when present
  • Device, when trusted
  • Task note, when added
  • Capture sequence

Retail Example

In a retail work lane, a worker may stock a shelf, face a display, scan a barcode, or capture a product photo. CyberJobs Work Capture keeps the process fast so the worker can continue the shift instead of stopping to enter the same details again.

Retail Fingerprint

Repeat the current store or assignment. Snap the shelf, product, barcode, or display. Next moves directly to the next item.

Service Example

In a service work lane, a worker may capture a repair, installation, cleanup, inspection, or before-and-after condition. CyberJobs Work Capture records the observation without turning the job into a paperwork process.

Service Fingerprint

Repeat the current job. Snap the work condition. Next moves directly to the next task or capture.
