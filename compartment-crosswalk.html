Truth Compartment Lane A — Intake + Bucket 1 — Sequester Raw truth enters unfiltered, gets logged, tagged, and preserved before interpretation. Truth survives before judgment. Raw arrival is not lost when processing fails.
Governance Compartment Override Lane — Pocket Chair / Board Human intervention freezes, annotates, approves, or redirects without deleting lineage. Authority becomes visible, bounded, and logged instead of silent or destructive.
Continuity Compartment Doctrine Layer — Governs All Lanes Identity and operating rules stay above implementation details. Doctrine survives even if a lane, Worker, bucket, or surface fails.
Creative Sovereignty — RETEN Lane B — Processing + Bucket 2 — Archive Creative artifacts, metadata, releases, and rights move through structured processing into canon. Artists retain sovereignty while releases remain traceable, versioned, and protected.
Resilience — Atomic Kricket Lane B — Processing + Archive Lineage Pressure-tested truth, lived experience, and failure signals become preserved artifacts. Collapse, recovery, and survival become records instead of erased events.
Public Surface Compartment Lane C — Surface / Pages / Surface API Public or role-gated surfaces read from approved canon and do not mutate truth. The surface stays stable while internal lanes repair, replay, or recover.
Intake Compartment Lane A — Arrival Protocol Incoming objects are accepted, rejected, queued, logged, and assigned lineage. Nothing enters without traceability. Intake remains dumb, durable, and loss-resistant.
Processing Compartment Lane B — Adjudication / Transformation Objects are transformed, enriched, classified, or failed into dead-letter paths. Processing is replayable, replaceable, and idempotent so truth is not corrupted.
Canon Compartment Bucket 2 — Archive / Canonical Truth Approved artifacts are stored as versioned, traceable, non-destructive records. Canon is immutable, lineage-bound, and available to surface lanes without becoming editable.

Operating Rule

No compartment may assume the duty of another compartment. No lane may silently rewrite another lane. No bucket may become a universal storage blob. No surface may mutate canon.

CyberCrowd survives because each compartment has a boundary, a role, and a failure radius.

small target fails whole environment survives

Macramé Principle

CyberCrowd is woven from tensioned compartments, not poured into one container. Each knot carries pressure without forcing the whole structure to collapse.

The crosswalk keeps the weave readable, repairable, and expandable.
