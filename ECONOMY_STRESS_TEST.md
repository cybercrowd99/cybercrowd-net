# ECONOMY_STRESS_TEST

## Purpose
The Economy Stress Test checks how the system handles heavy load, fast changes, and high pressure. It finds weak spots before they cause real trouble.

## What It Measures
- maximum load  
- reaction speed  
- weak areas  
- pressure points  
- recovery time  

## Why It Matters
Stress testing helps the system stay strong. It shows what needs fixing and how to prepare for tough situations.

## Summary
The stress test makes the economy safer by finding limits and improving them before they break.
