CYBERCROWD IDENTITY SESSION AUTHORITY MATRIX V1
Identity and session authority framework for CyberCrowd. This matrix defines how identity, devices, sessions, replay, and authority interact inside the operational lattice.
IDENTITY-001
Core Rule
Identity is not trust. Session is not authority. Device is not ownership. Authority must be assigned, validated, scoped, and replayable.

IDENTITY-004
Device Authority
Devices are assigned: - device ID - heartbeat lane - replay lane - session owner - authority scope A device cannot: - self-assign governance - self-assign creator authority - bypass SEQUESTER - write canon directly Devices may: - emit heartbeat pulses - maintain replay continuity - preserve session continuity - submit intake payloads
IDENTITY-005
Cross-Device Continuity
Cross-device continuity requires: - verified session owner - replay continuity ID - active heartbeat - lineage eligibility - governance-clear transfer A session may migrate devices without: - losing replay continuity - losing ownership continuity - losing event ancestry Magic Cursor continuity later attaches here.
IDENTITY-006
Replay Ownership
Replay records must contain: - replay ID - session owner - source lane - target lane - authority scope - heartbeat continuity - timestamp chain Replay reconstructs activity. Replay does not overwrite canon.
IDENTITY-007
Governance Scope
Governance authority may: - suspend sessions - quarantine devices - annotate canon - freeze replay lanes - force archival hold - trigger emergency containment Governance authority may not: - silently rewrite canon - erase lineage history - bypass replay registration - bypass audit recording
IDENTITY-008
Kill-Switch Authority
Emergency authority requires: - governance session - replay registration - audit logging - lineage preservation - mandatory DEADLETTER recording Emergency shutdown actions must remain: replayable traceable reviewable
IDENTITY-009
Expiration Rules
Anonymous sessions: short expiration Verified sessions: renewable Governance sessions: forced expiration Emergency sessions: forced destruction after archive Replay continuity may survive session expiration when lineage authority permits continuation.
IDENTITY-010
Final Rule
CyberCrowd does not trust: arrival, session, device, or role alone. Trust emerges from: identity + authority + lineage + replayability + containment + verification Every action must belong to: someone, some session, some device, and some replayable authority path.
