# GOVERNANCE_ENFORCEMENT_NET

## Purpose
The Governance Enforcement Net explains how rules are upheld and how the system responds when something breaks the rules.

## What It Handles
- rule violations  
- unsafe actions  
- broken processes  
- misuse or errors  
- repeated problems  

## How It Works
- detects rule breaks  
- stops harmful actions  
- corrects the issue  
- restores order  
- prevents repeat problems  

## Why It Matters
Without enforcement, rules mean nothing. The enforcement net keeps the system fair, stable, and safe.

## Summary
The enforcement net protects the system by catching rule breaks and restoring order quickly.
