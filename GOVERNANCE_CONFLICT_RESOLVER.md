# GOVERNANCE_CONFLICT_RESOLVER

## Purpose
The Governance Conflict Resolver explains how the system handles disagreements, collisions, or competing requests.

## What It Handles
- conflicting requests  
- rule collisions  
- priority clashes  
- unclear ownership  
- competing needs  

## How It Works
- detects the conflict  
- compares the needs  
- checks the rules  
- chooses the safest path  
- restores order  

## Why It Matters
Conflicts can freeze or break the system. The resolver keeps everything moving by solving problems quickly and fairly.

## Summary
The conflict resolver protects the system from confusion by settling disagreements in a clear and stable way.
