# CyberJobs Live Résumé Model

## Purpose

This file defines the CyberJobs live résumé model.

In CyberJobs, a worker’s résumé is not a PDF, upload, corporate document, or sales pitch.

A CyberJobs résumé is generated by deeds.

The worker clocks in, the live résumé becomes visible, the client reviews the verified work history, and the client sends a ping if they want the worker.

No bidding.  
No sales pressure.  
No “hire me” pitch.  

Résumé → ping → job.

---

## 1. Résumé Type

CyberJobs résumés are live work-history profiles.

They are built from:

- completed jobs
- verified work history
- camera uploads or proof
- skill tags
- rates
- boundaries
- guarantees
- repair windows
- refund rules
- availability
- trust score from past pings

The résumé becomes visible when the worker clocks in.

---

## 2. Dan’s Live Résumé

Dan’s CyberJobs résumé may include:

- past completed jobs
- camera uploads of each job
- skill tags, such as pool service tech
- boundaries showing what he does and does not do
- hourly or per-job rates
- guarantee terms
- repair windows
- refund policy
- availability window
- trust score from past pings

Dan clocks in.

His résumé becomes visible.

Clients review his résumé and camera uploads.

If a client wants Dan, the client sends a ping.

---

## 3. Jan’s Live Résumé

Jan’s CyberJobs résumé includes the same core structure as Dan’s résumé, with added security and boundary terms.

Jan’s résumé may include:

- clear rate card
- guarantee terms
- refund rules
- repair window
- camera uploads of past work
- boundaries
- skill tags
- availability window
- trust score
- security clause

Example security clause:

> Will not work inside a residence unaccompanied.

Jan clocks in.

Her résumé becomes visible.

Clients review her résumé, camera uploads, rates, and boundaries.

If a client wants Jan, the client sends a ping.

---

## 4. How Clients Use CyberJobs Résumés

Clients do three things:

1. Review the résumé.
2. Review the camera uploads or proof.
3. Send a ping if they want the worker.

CyberJobs does not require workers to beg, bid, chase, or sell themselves.

The work record speaks first.

---

## 5. Core Model

In CyberJobs, a worker’s résumé is generated by deeds, not uploads.

When Dan or Jan clock in, their live résumé becomes visible for clients to review.

That live résumé may include rates, boundaries, guarantees, verified job history, camera proof, availability, refund rules, repair windows, security clauses, and trust pings.

Clients review the work record first.

Then they send a ping.

No bidding.  
No sales pressure.  
No uploaded résumé theater.

---

## 6. Core Rule

CyberJobs résumés are earned by work, revealed by clock-in, and activated by ping.
