# ECONOMY_PRIORITY_LADDER

## Purpose
The Economy Priority Ladder explains how the system decides which needs come first when resources are limited. It keeps the system focused on what matters most.

## What It Ranks
- critical needs  
- safety needs  
- stability needs  
- performance needs  
- optional needs  

## How It Works
- checks what is most important  
- assigns a clear order  
- protects the top priorities  
- delays lower tasks  
- keeps the system stable  

## Why It Matters
When resources are tight, the system must choose wisely. The priority ladder makes sure the right things happen first.

## Summary
The priority ladder keeps the economy steady by choosing the best order for limited resources.
