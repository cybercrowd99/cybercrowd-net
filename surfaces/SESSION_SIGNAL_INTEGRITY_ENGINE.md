# SESSION SIGNAL INTEGRITY ENGINE
## Sovereign Identity Spine — Organ File

### Purpose
The Session Signal Integrity Engine ensures that every identity‑related signal entering
the session is authentic, untampered, and consistent with the established identity
frame. It prevents corruption, spoofing, drift‑masking, and synthetic signal injection.

This organ acts as the “immune system” of the identity spine.

---

### Core Responsibilities
- Validate the authenticity of every incoming identity signal  
- Detect tampering, corruption, or synthetic injection  
- Measure signal health, strength, and continuity  
- Compare signals against the stable identity frame  
- Reject or quarantine compromised signals  
- Maintain a real‑time integrity score for the session  

---

### Signal Classes Monitored
- **Primary Signals**  
  Facial telemetry, voiceprint, keystroke cadence, device posture, session entropy

- **Secondary Signals**  
  Peripheral resonance, environmental consistency, behavioral micro‑patterns

- **Tertiary Signals**  
  Low‑confidence indicators used only when primary and secondary weaken

---

### Integrity Evaluation Pipeline
1. **Capture**  
   All incoming signals are collected in raw form.

2. **Normalize**  
   Signals are standardized into comparable formats.

3. **Authenticate**  
   Each signal is checked for origin, continuity, and legitimacy.

4. **Cross‑Reference**  
   Signals are compared against the stable identity frame.

5. **Score**  
   Each signal receives an integrity score from 0–100.

6. **Fuse**  
   Scores are combined into a unified session integrity index.

7. **Act**  
   - High integrity → accept  
   - Medium integrity → monitor  
   - Low integrity → quarantine  
   - Critical integrity → escalate  

---

### Integrity Threat Detection
The engine detects:

- **Signal Drift**  
  Gradual deviation from expected patterns.

- **Signal Fracture**  
  Sudden breaks or contradictions.

- **Signal Spoofing**  
  Synthetic or replayed identity vectors.

- **Signal Injection**  
  Unauthorized signals entering the session.

- **Signal Masking**  
  Attempts to hide drift or contradictions.

---

### Integrity Enforcement Actions
- **Soft Reject**  
  Signal ignored but session continues.

- **Hard Reject**  
  Signal blocked and logged.

- **Quarantine**  
  Suspicious signals isolated for analysis.

- **Escalation Trigger**  
  Identity Escalation Engine activated.

- **Session Lock**  
  Session frozen until identity is re‑verified.

## Integrity Failure Modes
The engine classifies failures into four sovereign categories:

### 1. Drift‑Based Failure
Occurs when signals slowly deviate from the stable identity frame.
- Gradual pattern shift
- Behavioral cadence mismatch
- Weakening resonance alignment
- Increasing entropy variance

### 2. Fracture‑Based Failure
Occurs when signals suddenly contradict each other.
- Hard breaks in telemetry
- Sudden posture or cadence inversion
- Conflicting identity vectors
- Multi‑thread identity collision

### 3. Synthetic Failure
Occurs when signals show signs of artificial generation.
- Replay artifacts
- Latency‑inconsistent patterns
- Non‑human micro‑variations
- Perfect‑curve anomalies

### 4. Injection Failure
Occurs when unauthorized signals enter the session.
- Device mismatch
- Network origin inconsistency
- Unexpected signal class
- Unregistered resonance signature
