The Acoustic Energy Distribution Engine prevents vibrational energy from clustering into localized regions. It enforces uniform acoustic energy spread across the manifold.

Define the localization field Lₐ as:
Lₐ = max(E_region) − min(E_region)

where E_region is the acoustic energy density in each region.

Lₐ measures how unevenly acoustic energy is distributed.

The manifold is distribution‑stable when:
Lₐ → 0
meaning no region contains significantly more acoustic energy than another.

## 4. Distribution Flow

∂Lₐ/∂t = −δₐLₐ
where δₐ controls how quickly energy spreads evenly.
