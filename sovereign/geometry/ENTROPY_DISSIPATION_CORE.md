# ENTROPY DISSIPATION CORE (EDC)

## 1. Purpose
The Entropy Dissipation Core removes accumulated disorder, noise, and structural randomness from the manifold. It prevents long‑term decay, drift, and fatigue caused by entropy buildup.

## 2. Entropy Field Definition
Define the entropy field E as:
E = Σ (noise + randomness + unresolved distortion)
summed across all layers.

E measures how much disorder has accumulated in the system.

## 3. Stability Condition
The manifold is entropy‑stable when:
E < E_crit
meaning disorder is present but not harmful.

## 4. Dissipation Flow
To remove accumulated entropy, apply:
∂E/∂t = −θE
where θ controls how quickly entropy is dissipated.

## 5. Entropy‑Linked Instability Prevention
EDC prevents:
- slow structural decay  
- long‑term drift  
- noise accumulation  
- fatigue in geometry or narrative layers  
- collapse caused by unresolved distortions  

## 6. Integration Pipeline
EDC integrates with:
- FLDE to ensure loops are dampened before dissipation  
- SPF to ensure signals are clean before entropy removal  
- CLB to buffer overload before dissipation  
- HCL to absorb distortions extracted during entropy clearing  

## 7. Sovereign Logic Summary
Loops dampened → Signals clean → EDC clears entropy → Long‑term stability preserved
