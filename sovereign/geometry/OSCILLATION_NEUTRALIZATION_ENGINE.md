## 2. Oscillation Field Definition
Define the oscillation field Ω as:
Ω = amplitude × frequency

Ω measures the strength of cyclic geometric motion.

## 3. Stability Condition
The manifold is oscillation‑stable when:
Ω < Ω_crit
meaning oscillations exist but cannot grow or destabilize the structure.

## 4. Neutralization Flow
To suppress oscillation, apply:
∂Ω/∂t = −ξΩ
where ξ controls how quickly oscillatory motion is dampened.

- rhythmic geometric wobble  
- resonance between AFR and DCM  
- oscillatory collapse modes
