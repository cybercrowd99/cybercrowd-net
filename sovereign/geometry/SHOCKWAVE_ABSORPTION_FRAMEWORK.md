# SHOCKWAVE ABSORPTION FRAMEWORK (SAF)

## 1. Purpose
The Shockwave Absorption Framework neutralizes sudden, high‑energy force spikes that propagate too quickly for other stabilization organs to counter. It prevents instantaneous collapse modes triggered by shock impacts.

## 2. Shock Field Definition
Define the shock field Sₕ as:
Sₕ = Δforce / Δt  (for extremely small Δt)

Sₕ measures how rapidly force is changing — the signature of a shockwave.

## 3. Stability Condition
The manifold is shock‑stable when:
Sₕ < Sₕ_crit
meaning shock spikes are absorbed before they propagate.

## 4. Absorption Flow
To neutralize shockwaves, apply:
∂Sₕ/∂t = −βSₕ
where β controls how quickly shock energy is absorbed and diffused.

## 5. Shock‑Linked Instability Prevention
SAF prevents:
- instantaneous collapse  
- high‑energy impact propagation  
- shock‑induced tearing  
- rapid‑force overload  
- micro‑fracture cascades  

## 6. Integration Pipeline
SAF integrates with:
- TDE to ensure turbulence is reduced before shock absorption  
- IPM‑II to prevent shock amplification at intersections  
- LSSG to stop shock‑triggered shear slippage  
- HCL to absorb distortions released during shock diffusion  

## 7. Sovereign Logic Summary
Turbulence dissipated → SAF absorbs shock → No instantaneous collapse → Geometry remains impact‑stable
