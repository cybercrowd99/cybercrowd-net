CYBERCROWD ACCESS TIER AUTHORITY LAW V1
Authority and participation doctrine for CyberCrowd continuity systems. This document defines operational authority tiers, visibility boundaries, routing permissions, moderation permissions, replay permissions, and monetization scope.
ACCESS-001
Core Rule
Not every participant requires the same authority. CyberCrowd separates: visibility routing replay deployment moderation governance monetization and forensic access into governed authority tiers.

ACCESS-003
ADMIN Authority Scope
ADMIN may: - view operational health - govern continuity systems - review incidents - manage connector systems - manage forensic review - freeze routes - approve restoration - manage deployment lanes - manage replay infrastructure ADMIN may not: silently bypass replay silently erase evidence silently rewrite continuity history
ACCESS-004
CREATOR Authority Scope
CREATOR may: - create live continuity - route approved destinations - manage creator replay visibility - manage archive participation - manage monetization routes - manage creator audience continuity CREATOR may not: access unrelated forensic systems access governance authority access hidden connector credentials
ACCESS-005
MODERATOR Authority Scope
MODERATOR may: - moderate continuity spaces - review safety alarms - isolate abusive participation - trigger moderation freeze - escalate incidents MODERATOR may not: restore creator authority modify replay history control governance infrastructure
ACCESS-006
SPONSOR Authority Scope
SPONSOR may: - participate in sponsored routes - review sponsor analytics - manage approved sponsor campaigns - view contracted route metrics SPONSOR may not: control creator continuity access forensic continuity modify governance systems
ACCESS-007
VERIFIED_NODE Authority Scope
VERIFIED_NODE may: - participate in continuity routing - participate in heartbeat systems - participate in replay continuity - support approved continuity infrastructure VERIFIED_NODE may not: self-promote authority silently escalate trust restore quarantined continuity
ACCESS-008
MEMBER Authority Scope
MEMBER may: - participate in community continuity - join approved live spaces - interact with creator continuity - use approved routing surfaces - maintain personal continuity profile MEMBER may not: access operational continuity systems access forensic systems access governance systems
ACCESS-009
FREE ACCESS Scope
FREE_ACCESS may: - observe public continuity - access limited public spaces - access approved public events - access restricted creator previews FREE_ACCESS remains intentionally limited to protect operational continuity.
ACCESS-010
GUEST Scope
GUEST participation remains temporary. GUEST may: - view approved public content - access approved public links - access temporary continuity invitations GUEST may not: retain persistent continuity authority retain replay authority retain routing authority
ACCESS-011
SANDBOX Scope
SANDBOX exists for: testing simulation connector experiments continuity experiments route experiments SANDBOX may never: mutate Production continuity mutate Production replay mutate Production authority mutate Production monetization

ACCESS-013
Authority Escalation Rule
Authority escalation requires: - replay-visible authorization - trust verification - governance review if required - visible scope transition - recoverable audit trail Silent authority escalation is forbidden.
ACCESS-014
Tier Isolation Rule
A lower tier must not silently inherit higher authority. Examples: - FREE_ACCESS cannot inherit CREATOR authority - MEMBER cannot inherit ADMIN authority - VERIFIED_NODE cannot inherit GOVERNANCE authority - SANDBOX cannot inherit Production authority Isolation preserves continuity safety.
ACCESS-015
Final Rule
CyberCrowd continuity remains governable because authority is compartmentalized. Different participants require: different visibility different routing different replay access different operational power Authority without boundaries creates continuity instability.
