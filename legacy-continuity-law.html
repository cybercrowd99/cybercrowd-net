CYBERCROWD LEGACY CONTINUITY LAW V1
Legacy continuity doctrine for CyberCrowd. This document defines how human contribution, craft knowledge, creator identity, cultural participation, and educational value may continue beyond active participation.
LEGACY-001
Core Rule
Human contribution should not disappear quietly. CyberCrowd may preserve: knowledge craft creation teaching story method experience cultural motion as living continuity, not static memory.
LEGACY-002
Legacy Contribution Classes
Legacy Class Purpose Continuity Scope
craft legacy preserve trade knowledge educational continuity
creator legacy preserve artistic/creative contribution creator continuity
builder legacy preserve real-world build knowledge physical continuity
teacher legacy preserve instruction and guidance learning continuity
cultural legacy preserve story, community, meaning cultural continuity
technical legacy preserve design, CAD, systems, repair knowledge technical continuity
LEGACY-003
Living Knowledge Preservation
Legacy preservation may include: - video demonstrations - audio explanations - 3D lessons - VR training spaces - diagrams - CAD references - annotated procedures - apprenticeship archives - story interviews - live replay sessions Legacy should remain usable, not merely decorative.
LEGACY-004
Human Dignity Rule
Legacy continuity must preserve dignity. A contributor must not be reduced to: content inventory training data brand decoration or extracted memory. Legacy continuity exists to honor, teach, attribute, and sustain human contribution.
LEGACY-005
Real-World Continuity Symmetry
CyberCrowd recognizes continuity across: physical work manual work creative work industrial work digital work technical work service work educational work The wrench, the hammer, the CAD file, the lesson, the stage, and the code all belong to human continuity.
LEGACY-006
Legacy Revenue Rule
Legacy contribution may carry revenue continuity. Revenue may route to: - the contributor - designated beneficiaries - educational funds - preservation funds - agreed legacy routes Legacy revenue must remain: visible contracted reviewable and human-approved.
LEGACY-007
Archive Eligibility
Legacy material may become archive eligible when: - contributor consent exists - ownership scope is known - lineage is documented - revenue routing is defined if applicable - educational or cultural value is present - preservation conditions are clear Archive eligibility does not equal platform ownership.
LEGACY-008
Legacy Review Rule
Legacy systems should support review for: - attribution accuracy - contributor consent - ownership scope - cultural sensitivity - educational usefulness - revenue routing - archive suitability Legacy must remain accountable.
LEGACY-009
Digital Statue Rule
A digital statue is not a frozen idol. A CyberCrowd digital statue may be: - searchable knowledge - guided teaching - preserved craft - living demonstration - interactive lesson - contributor memory - cultural marker The purpose is continuity of human value.
LEGACY-010
Legacy Inheritance Connection
Legacy continuity may connect to: - creator inheritance routing - contract topology - continuity economics - archive systems - education systems - vocational continuity - beneficiary routing Inheritance details require separate governed law.
LEGACY-011
Forbidden Legacy Behavior
Forbidden: legacy extraction without consent Forbidden: hidden revenue capture Forbidden: erasing contributor attribution Forbidden: turning human knowledge into anonymous platform inventory Forbidden: using legacy systems to replace human dignity
LEGACY-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd legacy continuity exists because people matter. The larger cultural motion survives when human contribution remains visible, teachable, attributed, recoverable, and alive.
