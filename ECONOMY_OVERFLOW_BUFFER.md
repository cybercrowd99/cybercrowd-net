# ECONOMY_OVERFLOW_BUFFER

## Purpose
The Economy Overflow Buffer catches extra value or demand when levels rise too high. It protects the system from overload.

## What It Handles
- sudden spikes  
- extra demand  
- overflow pressure  
- unstable surges  
- temporary excess  

## How It Works
- stores extra load  
- releases it slowly  
- protects weak areas  
- prevents crashes  
- keeps flow steady  

## Why It Matters
Without a buffer, the system could overload during busy times. The buffer keeps everything safe and stable.

## Summary
The overflow buffer absorbs extra pressure so the economy stays balanced and strong.
