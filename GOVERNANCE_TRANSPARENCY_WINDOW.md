# GOVERNANCE_TRANSPARENCY_WINDOW

## Purpose
The Governance Transparency Window shows how the system makes decisions and takes action. It keeps everything open and easy to understand.

## What It Reveals
- actions taken  
- reasons behind choices  
- steps in the process  
- rule checks  
- final outcomes  

## Why It Matters
Transparency builds trust. When users can see how decisions are made, the system feels fair and reliable.

## Summary
The transparency window keeps governance open, honest, and clear for everyone.
