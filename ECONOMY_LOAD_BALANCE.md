# ECONOMY_LOAD_BALANCE

## Purpose
The Economy Load Balance module spreads out demand so no part of the system gets overwhelmed. It keeps pressure even and prevents overload.

## What It Handles
- rising demand  
- uneven pressure  
- heavy traffic  
- weak or stressed areas  
- sudden spikes  

## How It Works
- shifts load to open space  
- reduces pressure on busy parts  
- boosts slow areas  
- keeps value moving  
- prevents crashes  

## Why It Matters
Balanced load keeps the system fast, steady, and hard to break.

## Summary
Load balance protects the economy by spreading demand and keeping everything running smoothly.
