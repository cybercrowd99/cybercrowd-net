CYBERCROWD STUDIO ORCHESTRATION SURFACE LAW V1
STUDIO orchestration doctrine for CyberCrowd. This document defines how creators, operators, relays, replay systems, and continuity environments interact through the STUDIO orchestration surface.
STUDIO-001
Core Rule
STUDIO exists to orchestrate continuity. STUDIO should: launch route monitor preserve and sustain continuity movement without becoming hidden autonomous authority.

STUDIO-003
Live Route Orchestration Rule
STUDIO may orchestrate: - simultaneous live routing - relay launch sequencing - replay recording - audience convergence - continuity migration - route stabilization The creator remains: the initiating continuity authority.
STUDIO-004
Human-Governed Launch Rule
Critical continuity launch remains: human initiated. The system may: assist prepare verify monitor and recommend but final launch authority remains: human-governed.
STUDIO-005
Multi-Platform Continuity Rule
STUDIO may route continuity toward: - CyberCrowd - external live platforms - replay systems - sponsor systems - relay systems - archive continuity systems One continuity presence may exist across many destinations.
STUDIO-006
Replay Orchestration Rule
STUDIO may preserve: - replay chronology - launch chronology - relay chronology - migration chronology - route chronology - incident chronology Replay continuity supports: reconstruction preservation education and accountability.
STUDIO-007
Relay Visibility Rule
STUDIO should expose: - relay health - route degradation - audience convergence - replay state - stabilization state - continuity alarms - migration activity Operational visibility preserves trust.
STUDIO-008
Interruption-Aware Orchestration
STUDIO should preserve: human pacing ambient continuity sleep continuity passive continuity and accessibility continuity. Operational orchestration should avoid: weaponized interruption systems.
STUDIO-009
Replacement Continuity Rule
If continuity destabilizes: STUDIO may activate: - replacement relay routes - replacement replay systems - replacement continuity nodes - emergency continuity pathways The larger continuity ecosystem survives through orchestrated recovery.
STUDIO-010
Continuity Authority Boundary
STUDIO must not: silently seize creator authority silently reroute monetization silently erase replay continuity silently launch continuity silently escalate governance authority STUDIO assists continuity. STUDIO does not replace human governance.
STUDIO-011
Forbidden STUDIO Behavior
Forbidden: hidden launch authority Forbidden: silent routing manipulation Forbidden: concealed replay destruction Forbidden: cross-tier authority escalation Forbidden: forced interruption orchestration Forbidden: creator continuity seizure
STUDIO-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd STUDIO exists to: orchestrate preserve route and sustain living continuity movement. The larger cultural motion survives through human-governed continuity orchestration.
