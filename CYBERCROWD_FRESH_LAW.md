# CyberCrowd Fresh Law v1.0

## The Lateral Constitution of Rooms, Veils, Pings, and Movement

CyberCrowd is not a funnel.

CyberCrowd is a living structure of rooms, edges, gates, veils, pings, policies, and movement.

This law exists to stop collapse.

No room becomes another room.  
No public surface becomes private authority.  
No observer becomes enforcement.  
No money becomes truth.  
No helper becomes owner.  
No movement happens without declared permission.

CyberCrowd moves laterally.

Public asks.  
Private decides.  
The Gate proves human.  
The Profile establishes person.  
/VDio veils identity.  
Pepper pings.  
Secretary authorizes.  
Halo carries policy.  
Octopus moves.  
Turd removes money from judgment.

This is the operating constitution:

DOCTYPE: Lateral.  
Authority: Private.  
Observer: Read-Only.  
Movement: Declared.  
Identity: Veiled.  
Money: Stripped.

No collapse.  
No creep.  
No hidden ownership.

## 1. Prime Law

CyberCrowd survives by separation.

A thing may be near another thing without becoming that thing.

A page may point to a room without owning the room.

A route may coordinate movement without owning identity.

An observer may see risk without becoming the actor.

A muscle may move objects without deciding truth.

No collapse.  
No creep.  
No hidden ownership.

## 3. Gate Law

The Gate proves only one thing:

A human is present.

The Gate does not prove profile ownership.  
The Gate does not establish identity.  
The Gate does not mint EAT.  
The Gate does not create the full account.  
The Gate does not decide creator, viewer, worker, admin, buyer, or member status.

The Gate only allows the next setup movement to be considered.

No human pass = no setup token.

## 4. Public / Private Authority Law

Public asks.

Private decides.

Public files may request, display, collect browser-side tokens, and show movement feedback.

Public files may not own secrets, setup-token authority, password authority, EAT/session ownership, cookie ownership, profile establishment, or final pass/fail decisions.

Private auth Worker or server-side routes own the gate decision.

A public file may carry a request.

A private authority must decide whether the request may move.

## 5. Held-State Law

After the Gate passes and before the Profile is established, the person remains in held-state.

The person is human, but not fully assigned.

They are not automatically new.  
They are not automatically returning.  
They are not automatically viewer.  
They are not automatically creator.  
They are not automatically worker.  
They are not automatically buyer.  
They are not automatically admin.

The Profile establishment process collapses held-state into a known CyberCrowd person.

The ghost becomes a person only at Profile Collapse.

## 6. Selector Ladder Law

The ladder is not page order.

The ladder is selector order.

CyberCrowd movement follows:

wait -> selector -> wait -> selector -> wait -> selector

Each selector may collapse only the state it is authorized to collapse.

A selector does not own the whole person.  
A selector does not own the whole route.  
A selector does not own the whole system.
