CYBERCROWD LEDGER FORMULA SHEET

Operational lineage memory, CRWD propagation, dynamic payout rails, and governance override math.
The Ledger is Operational Lineage Memory.

1. Ledger Purpose

The CyberCrowd Ledger does not function as a bank, exchange, or market maker. It functions as a canonical operational memory layer that tracks cause, effect, propagation, settlement recognition, creator solvency, CRWD pressure, and platform survivability.

Ledger Role: - record native unit - record USD mirror at event time - preserve lineage - prevent hidden debt - support replay - support governance audit - prevent silent overrides

2. Ledger Rails

USD Rail

Tracks fiat-equivalent inflows, external payment gateway values, and audit mirrors.

CRWD Rail

Tracks native CRWD circulation, settlement recognition, and token pressure.

Subscription Rail

Tracks tier access, active service time, renewals, stops, and CRWD stream options.

Creator Payout Rail

Tracks accrued creator earnings and payout execution.

Node / Operator Rail

Tracks uptime verification, heartbeat rewards, compute credits, and operational incentives.

Governance Override Rail

Tracks administrative rule changes, overrides, treasury interventions, and audit receipts.

Archive Lineage Rail

Tracks data persistence, canon linkage, storage integrity, and long-term record cost.

AdWorm / Referral Rail

Tracks interaction credits, ad revenue events, and referral incentive balances.

3. Base Unit Rule

Every transaction is recorded in its native unit and mirrored into USD-equivalent value at the time of the event. CRWD remains floating by market behavior. The USD mirror is an audit and reporting layer, not a guaranteed redemption promise.

Native Record: amount_native unit_native = CRWD | USD | CREDIT | ACCESS | NODE Mirror Record: amount_usd_mirror = amount_native × market_rate_at_event_time Rule: native event survives even if mirror rate changes later

5. Dynamic Split Variables

Payout percentages are not hard-coded forever. They are dynamic governance variables that may be changed by authorized governance action.

χ = creator percentage ν = node/operator percentage α = archive/continuity percentage γ = governance/treasury percentage π = platform margin Golden Rule: χ + ν + α + γ + π = 1.00

If the sum does not equal 1.00, the ledger rejects the rule set.

6. Initial Split Logic

7. Payout Formulas

S_net = net subscription or settlement amount after external processing cost P_creator = χ × S_net P_platform = π × S_net P_node = ν × S_net P_archive = α × S_net P_gov = γ × S_net Total Distribution: P_creator + P_platform + P_node + P_archive + P_gov = S_net

8. Platform Equity Formula

Platform equity is the net increase in platform-controlled assets after all rail liabilities and operating costs are settled.

E_platform = Σ(S + R_a + CRWD_gain_usd_mirror) - Σ(P_creator + P_node + P_archive + P_gov + C_op + C_gas + C_slippage + C_chargeback) Where: S = subscription inflow R_a = AdWorm revenue C_op = operational cost C_gas = payout gas fee C_slippage = CRWD conversion slippage C_chargeback = failed payment / reversal cost

9. CRWD Velocity Formula

CRWD velocity tracks pressure on the CRWD rail by comparing total CRWD transaction volume to circulating supply.

V_c = Total Transaction Volume in CRWD ÷ Circulating Supply of CRWD Interpretation: Higher V_c = higher movement pressure Lower V_c = lower movement pressure V_c is observational. It is not a price guarantee.

11. Governance Override Rule

Governance can change variables only through logged override events. Changes apply to future transactions only. Past canonical payouts are not silently rewritten.

Governance Override Event: override_id admin_id old_rule_set new_rule_set timestamp reason effective_start_time signature_or_authority_reference Rule: override_event must be archived before rule activation
