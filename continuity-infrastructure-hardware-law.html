CYBERCROWD CONTINUITY INFRASTRUCTURE HARDWARE LAW V1
Physical continuity infrastructure doctrine for CyberCrowd. This document defines how hardware systems preserve, transport, protect, and sustain continuity across physical and digital operational environments.
HARDWARE-001
Core Rule
Continuity requires physical infrastructure. The relay, the cable, the rack, the PCB, the camera, the microphone, the accessibility device, the field node, and the power system all belong to continuity survivability.

HARDWARE-003
Replacement Continuity Node Rule
If hardware continuity fails: replacement continuity nodes may activate. Replacement hardware should preserve: - continuity lineage - replay lineage - route continuity - operational survivability - audience continuity The ecosystem should avoid: single-device dependency collapse.
HARDWARE-004
Hardware Compartmentalization
Hardware systems should remain compartmentalized. Examples: - replay hardware isolated from credential hardware - relay hardware isolated from governance systems - accessibility systems isolated from critical authority systems - field systems isolated from Production governance systems Compartmentalization preserves survivability.
