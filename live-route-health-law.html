CYBERCROWD LIVE ROUTE HEALTH LAW V1
Live route health doctrine for CyberCrowd continuity systems. This document defines how live continuity routes preserve: stability, visibility, migration survivability, relay synchronization, and audience continuity during active operational movement.
ROUTE-001
Core Rule
Live continuity routes should remain: observable recoverable migratable and survivable. Route health exists to preserve: continuity movement not merely connection status.

ROUTE-003
Continuity Degradation Rule
Continuity degradation may include: - relay instability - bandwidth fragmentation - replay instability - synchronization drift - audience fragmentation - route overload - node failure - emergency rerouting conditions The ecosystem should preserve: survivable continuity movement.
ROUTE-004
Relay Replacement Rule
If continuity destabilizes: replacement relays may activate. Replacement systems should preserve: - audience continuity - replay continuity - session continuity - route lineage - migration chronology The larger continuity ecosystem survives through replacement continuity pathways.
ROUTE-005
Route Visibility Rule
Operational systems should expose: - route stability - relay health - synchronization status - migration activity - restoration activity - replay state - audience continuity state Visibility preserves: operational trust.
ROUTE-006
Audience Continuity Preservation
Audience continuity should remain protected during: - relay migration - continuity rerouting - replay stabilization - regional instability - temporary degradation The ecosystem should minimize: audience continuity disruption.
ROUTE-007
Replay-Aware Monitoring
Replay systems may preserve: - route chronology - relay chronology - migration chronology - degradation chronology - restoration chronology - synchronization chronology Replay continuity supports: reconstruction analysis and survivability review.
ROUTE-008
Emergency Continuity Rule
Emergency continuity systems may: - reroute continuity - reduce route load - activate emergency relays - activate continuity preservation mode - isolate compromised continuity Emergency continuity exists to preserve: living operational movement.
ROUTE-009
Human Oversight Rule
Automation may: monitor recommend reroute or stabilize. Critical route authority remains: human-governed. The system assists continuity survivability. The system does not replace operational judgment.
ROUTE-010
Session Continuity Rule
Live sessions should survive: relay replacement route migration regional degradation temporary instability and replay recovery. The larger cultural motion survives through continuity persistence.
ROUTE-011
Forbidden Route Behavior
Forbidden: silent route destruction Forbidden: hidden migration manipulation Forbidden: concealed replay loss Forbidden: unrecoverable audience fragmentation Forbidden: single-point continuity dependency Forbidden: silent route authority seizure
ROUTE-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd live route systems exist to: preserve continuity movement protect audience presence sustain relay survivability and maintain operational continuity stability. The ecosystem remains alive through survivable continuity routing.
