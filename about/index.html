PING

The call that says, “Look here.” A person can PING a broken pump, a repair, a job need, a ride, a buyer, a camera angle, a lawn, an auto problem, a home task, or a skill that needs to be found. The PING does not command anyone. It shows the need, the place, and the opening so the right people can answer with the tool, contact, truck, fix, service, or help that moves the moment.

Value Cycle

In CyberCrowd, value never leaves the hands of the people who create it. Every action—credit, attention, help, or money—stays in motion inside the crowd that produced it. Nothing is siphoned upward, nothing disappears into a hidden system, and nothing is lost to an algorithmic drain. Value circulates where it was earned, and the crowd that builds it is the crowd that keeps it.

No tiny screwdriver required

Set Up

  1. Enter as yourself. You are already the playing piece.
  2. Open the lane where your thing can happen.
  3. CyberCrowd Positional Visibility Statement
    Show the Crowd the view from your position. What you can see is part of the shared map, and the map only exists when everyone contributes their line of sight.
  4. CyberCrowd Space-Making Statement
    Leave enough room in your lane for others to enter. Clearing space is how the Crowd expands, and expansion is how play continues.

Every turn begins with a view

Take Your Turn

  1. Look around

    What is happening?
    What is missing?
    What does everybody keep walking past?
    Show the world what you like!

  2. Open the Lane

    Open the lane where your thing can happen. Clear the clutter, widen the angle, give the moment a place to land—then ping your invites.

  3. Make it better

    Connect the people, tools, views, and needs in a way that did not exist five minutes ago.

  4. Send a PING

    Call the Crowd when the connection needs eyes, hands, questions, courage, noise, silence, job needs, repairs, services, lawn care, auto work, or domestic home services.

  5. Let somebody change it

    The next Identity does not have to repeat your move. They can answer, improve it, reroute it, soften it, sharpen it, or turn it into something nobody planned.

  6. The Crowd

    Not spectators trapped behind glass. Anyone who arrives can watch, answer, help, question, hire, critique, contribute, or bring the missing piece. The Crowd is the moment the room wakes up.

Everybody knows this player

What Spoils the Game

  • Stealing another Identity, lane, work, credit, or private life.
  • Forcing people into a hallway just to charge them for the exit.
  • Hiding the real move, the real price, the real punishment, or where the value went.
  • Turning the Crowd into inventory, targets, wallets, or behavioral livestock.
  • Destroying a person and calling it entertainment.
  • Using safety as a costume for control—or freedom as a costume for harm.

How to win

There Is No Final Square

You win whenever a connection creates more life, more time, more possibility, or more room than either side had before.

Find the point. Make the connection better. Let in the Crowd. Keep playing.
