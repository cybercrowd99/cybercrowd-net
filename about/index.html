One Identity to a Crowd

CYBERCROWD

How to Play

Bring what you know. Find what connects. Invent what comes next.

Open the box

Object of the game: Make the connection better. Invent another world from the people, skills, cameras, places, problems, and possibilities already in play.

Inside the box

Playing Pieces

●

Identity

That is you. Your work, your mess, your skill, your music, your need, your strange idea, and the things only you have seen.

▰

Lane

Your place to do the thing. A kitchen, job, show, repair, invention, camera view, stage, shop, or anything else you can actually move.

⌁

The Crowd

Not spectators trapped behind glass. They can watch, answer, help, question, hire, critique, contribute, or bring the missing piece.

!

PING

The call that says, “Look here.” Send one when you find a need, a person, a moment, or a connection worth moving.

◉

WDIG

The live wire. Plug in phones, drones, VR rigs, strange cameras, and different views of the same moment.

↻

Value Cycle

What people add keeps moving near the people who added it. Credit, attention, help, and money do not vanish down a hidden drain.

No tiny screwdriver required

Set Up

  1. Enter as yourself. You are already the playing piece.
  2. Open the lane where your thing can happen.
  3. Show the Crowd what you can see from where you stand.
  4. Leave enough room for another Identity to change the picture.

Every turn begins with a view

Take Your Turn

  1. Look around

    What is happening? What is missing? What does everybody keep walking past?

  2. Find the connection

    A plumber may also build impossible hot tubs. A viewer may have the perfect camera angle. A grandmother may need work. A garage may become a stage.

  3. Make it better

    Connect the people, tools, views, and needs in a way that did not exist five minutes ago.

  4. Send a PING

    Call the Crowd when the connection needs eyes, hands, questions, courage, noise, silence, or one very particular weirdo.

  5. Let somebody change it

    The next Identity does not have to repeat your move. They can answer, improve it, reroute it, soften it, sharpen it, or turn it into something nobody planned.

Use when ordinary quits working

Wild Cards

✦

Magic Cursor

Change the view. Reveal the door, person, object, or possibility everybody else missed.

★

Apogee Wild Card

Play when the ordinary route is dead and the ridiculous route is starting to look intelligent.

♜

Operator Move

Run the lane. Choose the views. Invite the Crowd. Keep the moment moving without owning the people inside it.

➶

adWorm

Let a brand enter only when it adds value to the lane. The worm follows the moment—not the human home.

Everybody knows this player

What Spoils the Game

  • Stealing another Identity, lane, work, credit, or private life.
  • Forcing people into a hallway just to charge them for the exit.
  • Hiding the real move, the real price, the real punishment, or where the value went.
  • Turning the Crowd into inventory, targets, wallets, or behavioral livestock.
  • Destroying a person and calling it entertainment.
  • Using safety as a costume for control—or freedom as a costume for harm.

How to win

There Is No Final Square

You win whenever a connection creates more life, more time, more possibility, or more room than either side had before.

Find the point. Make the connection better. Let in the Crowd. Keep playing.
