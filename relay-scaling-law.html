CYBERCROWD RELAY SCALING LAW V1
Relay scaling doctrine for CyberCrowd continuity systems. This document defines how continuity relays distribute, expand, stabilize, reroute, and preserve cultural motion during high operational demand.
RELAY-001
Core Rule
Relays exist to preserve continuity movement. A relay should: extend continuity distribute continuity protect continuity reroute continuity without becoming a hidden centralized choke point.
RELAY-002

Relay Class Purpose Operational Scope

RELAY-003
Relay Expansion Rule
Relay scaling may activate: - additional continuity lanes - regional relay clusters - backup transport paths - audience distribution relays - replay distribution relays - emergency stabilization relays Scaling should remain: replay-visible reviewable recoverable
RELAY-004
Regional Distribution Rule
Regional continuity should distribute operational pressure. The ecosystem should avoid: single-region dependency single-node dependency single-route dependency Continuity should remain geographically survivable.
RELAY-005
Replay Preservation Rule
Relay systems must preserve: - route chronology - relay activation chronology - relay migration chronology - degradation chronology - emergency reroute chronology Relay scaling must not erase replay continuity.
RELAY-006
Relay Degradation Handling
If a relay degrades: - mark relay health degraded - reroute continuity if possible - preserve replay chronology - preserve creator continuity - preserve audience continuity - preserve alarm visibility Degradation should become visible, not hidden.
RELAY-007
Relay Containment Rule
Compromised or unstable relays may: - isolate independently - freeze independently - downgrade independently - enter SEQUESTER - enter DECchamber if required One compromised relay must not collapse the larger continuity fabric.
RELAY-008
Replacement Relay Continuity
Replacement relays may activate when: - overload occurs - compromise occurs - instability occurs - geographic failure occurs - transport failure occurs Replacement continuity should: preserve route lineage preserve replay lineage preserve audience continuity
RELAY-009
Relay Stabilization Rule
Stabilization may: - reduce route pressure - reduce non-essential synchronization - reduce cosmetic traffic - prioritize creator continuity - prioritize replay continuity - prioritize governance visibility The larger cultural motion survives through continuity prioritization.
RELAY-010
Relay Visibility Rule
Relay systems should expose: - relay health - degradation state - route pressure - reroute state - emergency relay activation - stabilization state Operational visibility supports trust.
RELAY-011
Forbidden Relay Behavior
Forbidden: silent relay collapse Forbidden: hidden rerouting Forbidden: relay replay destruction Forbidden: cross-environment relay bleed Forbidden: relay authority escalation without approval Forbidden: single relay dependency architecture
RELAY-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd continuity survives scale through distributed relay movement. Relays exist to: extend continuity protect continuity reroute continuity and preserve cultural motion while the ecosystem remains operationally governable.
