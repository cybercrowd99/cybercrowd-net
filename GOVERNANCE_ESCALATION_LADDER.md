# GOVERNANCE_ESCALATION_LADDER

## Purpose
The Governance Escalation Ladder explains how issues rise through levels when they cannot be solved at the lower layer.

## What It Handles
- unresolved problems  
- blocked decisions  
- rule conflicts  
- authority gaps  
- high‑risk situations  

## How It Works
- start at the lowest level  
- attempt local resolution  
- escalate only if needed  
- assign higher authority  
- confirm final outcome  

## Why It Matters
Escalation keeps the system from getting stuck and ensures serious issues reach the right decision‑makers.

## Summary
The escalation ladder moves problems upward in a clear, controlled way so the system stays organized and responsive.
