# CyberCrowd A.R.C. Template

## Arrival → Recognition → Continuity

A.R.C. is the CyberCrowd room experience template.

It protects the user journey from becoming a dead menu, a broken form, or a restart loop.

Every major CyberCrowd room must answer three questions:

Arrival: How does the person enter?  
Recognition: What does the system understand now?  
Continuity: How does the person return without starting over?

---

## 1. Arrival

Arrival is the first feeling of entering a room.

Arrival is not only navigation.

Arrival is atmosphere, doorway, temperature, sound, motion, and invitation.

For CyberCrowd, Arrival may be:

- digital rain
- vault entry
- glass room
- cold outside / warm inside feeling
- Stark interior
- doorman presence
- first visible signal that CyberCrowd is alive

Arrival answers:

How did the person get here?  
What world did they just step into?  
What is the room inviting them to do?

Arrival must not own private authority.

Arrival may invite.

Arrival may display.

Arrival may point sideways.

Arrival may not decide identity, session, gate pass, profile, or money truth.

---

## 2. Recognition

Recognition is the moment CyberCrowd understands something about the person or object.

Recognition is not ownership.

Recognition is a declared state becoming visible.

Examples:

A human is present.  
An email was entered.  
A setup token returned.  
A password lane is ready.  
A password and verification match.  
A profile is incomplete.  
A member is returning.  
A session exists.  
A room is available.

Recognition answers:

What does the system know now?  
Who or what is being recognized?  
Which selector is allowed to collapse this state?  
What must remain veiled?

Recognition must stay bounded.

The Gate recognizes human presence only.

The setup room recognizes a returned setup state only.

The Profile establishes the person later.

Pepper may ping recognition.

Pepper may not own recognition.

Private authority decides recognition.

---

## 3. Continuity

Continuity is the return path.

Continuity prevents the user from being forced to restart after they already passed through a room.

Continuity is what lets the person come back.

Examples:

Returning member path.  
Login room.  
Session receipt.  
Dashboard return.  
Saved state.  
Expired session recovery.  
New setup link only when needed.  
No accidental Create Account loop.

Continuity answers:

How does this person return later?  
What happens if they already completed setup?  
What happens if their session expired?  
What happens if they try to sign in again?  
What path prevents confusion?

Continuity is required because the first CyberCrowd experience is meant to be memorable.

If the room creates a wow moment, the person will want to return.

The return door must be clean.

---

## 4. A.R.C. Rule

Every CyberCrowd room must pass the A.R.C. check before it is treated as complete.

A room is incomplete if it has Arrival but no Recognition.

A room is incomplete if it has Recognition but no Continuity.

A room is dangerous if it has Continuity by forcing the person back through first-time setup.

A room is broken if the user wants to return and cannot.

## 6. Setup Room A.R.C.

setup.html is not just a password form.

setup.html is the identity activation room.

Arrival: the user returns from the email link into the setup image.  
Recognition: the setup token is present and password lanes are ready.  
Continuity: after activation, the person must have a clean way back in later.

The setup room may use:

- glass over layers
- air typing
- ID lane
- forced password lane
- verify lane
- Activate Identity
- adWorm awakening
- circuit run
- voltage sizzle
- identity circuit locked

The setup room may not own:

- the Gate
- email sending
- Turnstile secret
- final EAT/session authority
- profile interview
- Pepper authority
- storefront behavior

## 7. adWorm Awakening Rule

adWorm awakening is a visual movement effect.

It is not advertising.

It is not tracking.

It is not authority.

adWorm awakening may show that a declared movement path is waking up.

In setup.html:

button spark  
circuit crawl  
lane trace  
identity pulse  
seal lock

The circuit may celebrate.

The circuit may not decide.

Private route authority still decides.

---

## 8. Return Loop Law

If a user experiences the CyberCrowd activation moment and wants to sign in again, the system must not force them through Create Account again.

Returning users need a clean continuity door.

The return door must preserve the feeling without repeating first-time setup.

A.R.C. requires:

First-time path creates wonder.  
Recognition creates identity.  
Continuity creates return.

---

## 9. A.R.C. Closing

A.R.C. is the template:

Arrival creates wonder.  
Recognition creates identity.  
Continuity creates return.

No CyberCrowd room is complete until all three are accounted for.
