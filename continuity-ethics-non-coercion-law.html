CYBERCROWD CONTINUITY ETHICS NON COERCION LAW V1
Ethical containment doctrine for CyberCrowd continuity systems. This document defines what continuity, replay, transport, accessibility, and distributed node systems must never become.
ETHICS-001
Core Rule
CyberCrowd continuity must remain human-serving. Continuity must never become: coercion, hidden surveillance, forced participation, silent authority escalation, or invisible control. The system may assist. The system may not manipulate.
ETHICS-002
Anti-Coercion Rule
No CyberCrowd system may pressure a user into: - pairing a device - enabling transport - joining replay continuity - allowing heartbeat participation - accepting remote control - uploading creator content - entering accessibility continuity - remaining in a session Participation must remain voluntary, visible, and reversible.
ETHICS-003
Anti-Hidden-Surveillance Rule
CyberCrowd must not use continuity systems for hidden surveillance. Forbidden: - invisible device tracking - covert fingerprinting - hidden clipboard access - hidden camera access - hidden microphone access - hidden cursor monitoring - hidden cross-device tracking - undisclosed replay recording Continuity records exist for recovery, traceability, and user protection, not exploitation.
ETHICS-004
Anti-Forced-Participation Rule
Users must be able to: - decline pairing - decline replay participation - decline transport participation - decline accessibility continuity - decline device migration - exit observer participation - revoke creator continuity - freeze or disconnect sessions No user should be trapped inside continuity.
ETHICS-005
Anti-Silent-Authority-Escalation Rule
Authority may not silently escalate. A device may not become: - creator authority - governance authority - archive authority - canon authority - transport authority without explicit verified scope. Trust escalation requires evidence. Authority escalation requires review.
ETHICS-006
Anti-Dark-Pattern Transport Rule
Transport systems must not trick users. Forbidden: - confusing consent buttons - hidden auto-enable behavior - misleading pairing language - unclear remote-control state - disguised clipboard permissions - forced migration prompts - fake urgency to approve access - difficult disconnect paths Transport must be understandable before it becomes active.
ETHICS-007
Replay Ethics
Replay exists to preserve continuity, recover failures, support review, and protect participants. Replay must not become: - secret surveillance - punishment without review - hidden behavior scoring - manipulation engine - coercive monitoring Replay visibility and review rules must remain clear.
ETHICS-008
Accessibility Dignity Rule
Accessibility systems must preserve dignity. Deaf Companion, Slate, speech bridge, caption systems, haptic continuity, and blind-mode systems must remain: human-first transparent consent-based interruptible non-exploitative Accessibility must not become dependency capture.
ETHICS-009
Observer Protection
Observers must not be converted into hidden infrastructure. Observer nodes may participate only with visible scope. Forbidden: - hidden relay use - invisible heartbeat use - silent replay enrollment - hidden transport participation - undisclosed device use Observers remain people, not passive infrastructure.
ETHICS-010
Creator Protection
Creators must retain clear control over: - uploads - lineage - archive eligibility - replay visibility - public release - cross-device continuity - collaboration scope Creator continuity must protect ownership, not dissolve it into platform control.
ETHICS-011
Node Ethics
Distributed nodes must remain scoped. Old phones, tablets, laptops, relay devices, and VR nodes may support continuity, but must not become hidden authority systems. Cheap hardware is a resource. Human control remains the law.
ETHICS-012
Governance Ethics
Governance exists to preserve trust, not to hide power. Governance actions must be: recorded reviewable replayable explainable Governance may freeze harmful behavior. Governance may not silently rewrite history.
ETHICS-013
Non-Coercive Recovery
Recovery systems must not force users back into continuity. Recovery may offer: - restore session - resume migration - reconnect device - recover replay - reclaim session Recovery must also allow: - stop - disconnect - abandon session - revoke continuity - archive and close
ETHICS-014
Human Override Ethics
Human override must remain stronger than automation. The universal stop command: "1" must be respected as: freeze, preserve, stop propagation, and protect continuity evidence. The system must not fight the stop.
ETHICS-015
Final Rule
CyberCrowd continuity is ethical only when: people understand it, people can stop it, people can leave it, people can review it, and people remain in control. Continuity without consent is not continuity. It is capture. CyberCrowd chooses continuity, not capture.
