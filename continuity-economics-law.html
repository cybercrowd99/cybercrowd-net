CYBERCROWD CONTINUITY ECONOMICS LAW V1
Continuity economics doctrine for CyberCrowd. This document defines how value, participation, contribution, market continuity, and ecosystem survivability move through the larger continuity fabric.
ECON-001
Core Rule
Economics should preserve living continuity. Economic systems should: support participation support contribution support survivability support transparency support cultural motion without trapping participants inside extractive systems.
ECON-002
Continuity Contribution Principle
Contribution may include: - physical labor - digital labor - creative labor - infrastructure labor - educational labor - accessibility contribution - relay participation - moderation participation - continuity stewardship All real continuity contribution matters.

ECON-004
Dynamic Ecosystem Assistance
CyberCrowd may provide: - market guidance - continuity-aware pricing insight - route friction insight - participation visibility - listing freshness assistance - continuity movement analysis - replay-visible recommendation systems The ecosystem should assist: not secretly manipulate.
ECON-005
Continuity-Aware Marketplace Rule
Marketplace systems should preserve: - active continuity - listing freshness - participation visibility - continuity movement - replay-visible changes - user-controlled permanence Dead continuity should: downgrade archive reroute or migrate into adworm systems.
ECON-006
Replay-Visible Economics
Replay systems may preserve: - participation chronology - continuity movement chronology - sponsorship chronology - route chronology - migration chronology - restoration chronology Economics should remain: observable reviewable recoverable
ECON-007
Continuity Survivability Rule
Economic continuity should survive: - route migration - relay replacement - regional instability - temporary outages - creator rerouting - infrastructure replacement The ecosystem should avoid: single-point economic collapse.
ECON-008
Storage Sovereignty Rule
Participants may: - self-store continuity - export continuity - route continuity externally - synchronize continuity intentionally - preserve local ownership CyberCrowd continuity does not require: permanent centralized storage ownership.
ECON-009
Human Continuity Parity Rule
No continuity role shall become diminished solely because it is: physical manual creative industrial digital or infrastructural. The ecosystem recognizes: real human continuity contribution.
ECON-010
LECmultiplier Principle
LECmultiplier represents: living ecosystem continuity multiplication. Contribution may multiply through: participation education preservation collaboration continuity movement and cultural motion. The ecosystem should amplify: human value continuity.
ECON-011
Forbidden Economic Behavior
Forbidden: hidden economic manipulation Forbidden: trap-based monetization Forbidden: forced continuity lock-in Forbidden: concealed sponsorship routing Forbidden: silent value extraction Forbidden: destroying continuity ownership visibility
ECON-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd economics exist to: connect support preserve and sustain living continuity participation. The ecosystem remains alive through continuity-aware movement, not static extraction systems. The larger cultural motion survives.
