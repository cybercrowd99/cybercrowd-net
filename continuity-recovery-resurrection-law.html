CYBERCROWD CONTINUITY RECOVERY RESURRECTION LAW V1
Recovery doctrine for CyberCrowd continuity systems. This document defines how continuity survives interruption, damage, collapse, corruption, migration failure, and partial system loss.
RECOVERY-001
Core Rule
Continuity may degrade. Truth must survive. The system must preserve: - replay evidence - heartbeat evidence - lineage continuity - governance continuity - authority history - recovery visibility Recovery must never silently rewrite history.
RECOVERY-002
Degraded Continuity States
State Description Allowed Behavior Recovery Route
stable continuity healthy normal operation none
degraded partial continuity instability restricted transport REPLAY reconstruction
frozen continuity intentionally paused preserve state only manual recovery
orphaned session lost active owner read-only reconstruction reclaim review

RECOVERY-003
Recovery Triggers
Recovery sequence begins when: - migration collapses - heartbeat chain breaks - replay chain corrupts - node disappears - dead battery occurs - session ownership lost - device destroyed - transport freezes unexpectedly - replay reconstruction mismatch occurs - relay node collapses
RECOVERY-004
Replay Reconstruction
Replay reconstruction may restore: - last known session state - cursor continuity - migration continuity - active surface - transport freeze state - trust scope - continuity ancestry Replay reconstruction may not: rewrite canon rewrite lineage rewrite governance history Replay reconstructs truth. Replay does not invent truth.
RECOVERY-005
Heartbeat Recovery
Heartbeat recovery verifies: - node reappearance - continuity timing - stale continuity age - resumed liveness - heartbeat consistency - node identity continuity Heartbeat recovery may downgrade trust before restoration resumes.
RECOVERY-006
Orphan Session Handling
A session becomes orphaned when: - active node disappears - migration incomplete - ownership unresolved - continuity cannot resume safely Orphan sessions: - freeze transport - preserve replay - preserve heartbeat evidence - preserve authority history - require reclaim confirmation No silent reassignment.
RECOVERY-007
Abandoned Node Handling
Abandoned nodes: - lose active authority - retain replay history - retain lineage references - retain quarantine history - may require re-registration Long-abandoned nodes may downgrade: trust scope transport eligibility continuity participation
RECOVERY-008
Authority Restoration Rules
Authority restoration requires: - replay verification - heartbeat verification - fingerprint continuity - trust review - migration review if applicable Restoration does not automatically: restore governance authority restore archive authority restore unrestricted transport High authority requires review.
RECOVERY-009
Quarantine Recovery
Quarantined continuity may recover only after: - replay audit - fingerprint audit - transport audit - heartbeat audit - governance review Recovery routes: SEQUESTER → review → restricted restoration → controlled continuity resume
RECOVERY-010
Partial Continuity Survival
Partial survival means: some continuity survives even if part fails. Examples: - replay survives but heartbeat fails - heartbeat survives but cursor fails - lineage survives but transport freezes - session survives but device destroyed The system must preserve: whatever truth still exists.
RECOVERY-011
Continuity Resurrection
Continuity resurrection means: a destroyed or interrupted session safely resumes. Requirements: - replay continuity exists - lineage continuity exists - trust scope verified - session reclaim verified - heartbeat restored - transport frozen until safe Resurrection is reconstruction, not history replacement.
RECOVERY-012
Recovery Visibility Rule
All recovery events must remain: replay-visible review-visible traceable timestamped containable Invisible recovery is forbidden.
RECOVERY-013
Emergency Stop Preservation
Emergency stop command: "1" must preserve: - replay evidence - heartbeat evidence - lineage continuity - governance continuity - recovery state - freeze state Emergency stop is preservation, not destruction.
RECOVERY-014
Final Rule
CyberCrowd continuity survives because damage does not erase truth. Devices may fail. Sessions may freeze. Nodes may disappear. But replay, heartbeat, lineage, and governance preserve the path back.
