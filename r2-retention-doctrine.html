CYBERCROWD R2 RETENTION DOCTRINE
Canonical retention and survivability authority for CyberCrowd operational infrastructure. Retention policy exists to preserve continuity, prevent silent loss, and isolate replaceable systems from canonical systems.
RETENTION-001
Canonical Ledger Events
Bucket: cybercrowd-ledger-events Retention: Permanent Deletion: Forbidden Replay: Required Survivability: Maximum Purpose: Canonical append-only operational truth. Rules: - never overwrite - never mutate - never compact silently - replay integrity mandatory - corruption creates deadletter copy
RETENTION-002
Deadletter Isolation
Bucket: cybercrowd-deadletter Retention: 30-90 days Deletion: Allowed after expiration Replay: Optional Survivability: Medium Purpose: Quarantine malformed, corrupt, or rejected payloads. Rules: - never route back into canonical chain automatically - isolate contamination - preserve forensic visibility temporarily
RETENTION-003
Replay Infrastructure
Bucket: cybercrowd-replay Retention: Long-term Deletion: Restricted Replay: Core requirement Survivability: High Purpose: Operational reconstruction, simulation, timeline replay, and survivability validation. Rules: - replay chain must remain reconstructable - replay gaps generate alerts
RETENTION-004
Archive Authority
Bucket: cybercrowd-archive Retention: Permanent Deletion: Forbidden except legal authority workflow Replay: Supported Survivability: Maximum Purpose: Canonical historical continuity archive. Rules: - immutable preservation - historical snapshots preserved - lineage references preserved - archive never treated as cache
RETENTION-005
Lineage Preservation
Bucket: cybercrowd-lineage Retention: Permanent Deletion: Forbidden Replay: Supported Survivability: Maximum Purpose: Ownership, provenance, inheritance, and continuity authority. Rules: - never silently modify ownership trails - preserve attribution history - preserve inheritance references
RETENTION-006
Governance Records
Bucket: cybercrowd-governance Retention: Permanent Deletion: Restricted Replay: Required Survivability: Maximum Purpose: Override logging, treasury actions, administrative intervention, and audit survivability. Rules: - all overrides logged - no silent governance mutation - replay required for disputes
RETENTION-007
Node Heartbeats
Bucket: cybercrowd-node-heartbeats Retention: 30-180 days Deletion: Allowed after expiration Replay: Partial Survivability: Medium Purpose: Mesh telemetry, latency tracking, device uptime, and operational health monitoring. Rules: - summarized historical metrics may survive - raw telemetry may expire
RETENTION-008
Surface Cache
Bucket: cybercrowd-surface-cache Retention: Short-term Deletion: Expected Replay: Disabled Survivability: Low Purpose: Accelerated public delivery cache. Rules: - cache is replaceable - never canonical - rebuild allowed - purge allowed
RETENTION-009
Graphics + Teasers
Buckets: cybercrowd-graphics cybercrowd-teasers Retention: Variable Deletion: Allowed Replay: Optional Survivability: Medium Purpose: Public overlays, graphics, teasers, and promotional media. Rules: - public media may rotate - canonical versions belong in archive
RETENTION-010
Long Form Media
Bucket: cybercrowd-album Retention: Long-term Deletion: Restricted Replay: Supported Survivability: High Purpose: Creator media preservation and continuity recordings. Rules: - public copies may rotate - master copies preserved - archive snapshots recommended
