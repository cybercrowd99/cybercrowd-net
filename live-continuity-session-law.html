CYBERCROWD LIVE CONTINUITY SESSION LAW V1
Live continuity doctrine for CyberCrowd operational systems. This document defines how live sessions preserve: presence, participation, relay continuity, replay survivability, ambient continuity, and distributed continuity movement.
SESSION-001
Core Rule
A live continuity session is a living continuity environment. Sessions should preserve: presence participation relay continuity replay continuity human pacing and operational survivability.

SESSION-003
Continuity Persistence Rule
The user may intentionally authorize: persistent continuity sessions. Persistent continuity may include: - ambient playback - long-form broadcasts - sleep continuity - educational continuity - healing continuity - passive participation continuity Continuity should not require constant interruption to remain valid.
SESSION-004
Passive Continuity Authorization
Passive participation remains valid. A session may continue while the participant: rests reads studies works sleeps travels or remains silently present within continuity space. Presence itself may hold value.
SESSION-005
Live Relay Orchestration
Sessions may route through: - relay continuity systems - STUDIO orchestration - replay continuity systems - multi-platform routing - emergency continuity relays - continuity replacement routes The ecosystem should preserve: session survivability.
SESSION-006
Replay-Linked Session Rule
Replay systems may preserve: - session chronology - launch chronology - relay chronology - audience chronology - interruption chronology - migration chronology - restoration chronology Replay continuity supports: preservation education and accountability.
SESSION-007
Continuity Migration Rule
If a live route destabilizes: sessions may migrate through: - replacement relays - replacement nodes - replacement replay systems - emergency continuity routes The larger continuity ecosystem survives through continuity migration.
SESSION-008
Interruption-Aware Session Rule
Sessions should avoid: - forced interruption prompts - volume shock advertisements - aggressive engagement interruptions - rage bait engagement loops - continuity invalidation prompts Human pacing matters. Rest is also human continuity.
SESSION-009
Ambient Continuity Rule
Ambient continuity sessions may support: - waterfalls - rainfall - healing audio - hotel ambience - restaurant continuity - office continuity - study continuity - low-motion atmosphere continuity The ecosystem may preserve: calm continuity environments.
SESSION-010
Session Authority Rule
The creator or continuity authority may: launch pause restore migrate or terminate continuity sessions. The platform must not: silently hijack continuity authority.
SESSION-011
Forbidden Session Behavior
Forbidden: weaponized interruption Forbidden: forced clickbait engagement Forbidden: sleep-disruptive monetization Forbidden: passive continuity invalidation Forbidden: silent session seizure Forbidden: destroying replay continuity without review
SESSION-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd live continuity sessions exist to: preserve participation preserve atmosphere preserve continuity movement and sustain human-centered operational presence. The larger cultural motion survives even while the human rests.
