CYBERCROWD CREATOR INHERITANCE ROUTING LAW V1
Creator inheritance routing doctrine for CyberCrowd. This document defines how creator continuity, legacy revenue, archive access, educational value, and beneficiary routing may survive beyond active participation.
INHERITANCE-001
Core Rule
Creator continuity may outlive active participation. Inheritance routing exists to preserve: ownership clarity beneficiary intent legacy revenue archive dignity educational continuity and human contribution without turning legacy into platform ownership.
INHERITANCE-002
Inheritance Classes
Inheritance Class Purpose Review Requirement
beneficiary routing route legacy revenue to designated recipient required
archive inheritance preserve archive access or stewardship required
educational inheritance preserve teaching/course continuity required
creator identity stewardship protect creator name and attribution required
contract inheritance continue approved contract obligations contract review
inactive creator continuity preserve non-active creator legacy periodic review
INHERITANCE-003
Beneficiary Designation Rule
A creator may designate beneficiaries for: - legacy revenue - educational continuity - archive stewardship - preservation decisions - contributor recognition - agreed continuity routes Beneficiary designation must be: intentional documented reviewable revocable while creator authority remains active
INHERITANCE-004
Legacy Revenue Routing
Legacy revenue may route through: - contributor account - designated beneficiary - educational fund - preservation fund - contract-defined route - estate-approved route Revenue routing must remain: visible accountable contracted and replay-visible.
INHERITANCE-005
Archive Continuity Routing
Archive continuity may preserve: - creator works - lessons - demonstrations - live replays - craft knowledge - cultural documentation - continuity records Archive inheritance defines who may: review maintain release restrict or retire legacy material.
INHERITANCE-006
Creator Identity Stewardship
Creator identity must remain protected. Inheritance routing must not: erase attribution misrepresent the creator falsely imply new consent silently commercialize identity detach work from source lineage Legacy should preserve human dignity.
INHERITANCE-007
Consent Boundary Rule
Creator consent remains central. Inheritance routing must respect: - creator instructions - contract scope - archive restrictions - privacy preferences - cultural sensitivity - beneficiary designation No inheritance route should invent consent after the fact.
INHERITANCE-008
Dispute Containment
If inheritance conflict occurs: - freeze disputed routing - preserve replay chronology - preserve contract chronology - preserve revenue chronology - preserve archive state - route dispute to governance review Disputed continuity should not silently continue.
INHERITANCE-009
Inactive Creator Continuity
Inactive creator continuity may enter: - preservation mode - archive mode - educational mode - restricted mode - review mode Inactive does not mean abandoned. Continuity should be evaluated with dignity.
INHERITANCE-010
Replay Visibility Rule
Replay may preserve: - designation chronology - revenue routing chronology - archive access chronology - contract transition chronology - dispute chronology - governance review chronology Replay supports accountability. Replay must not expose private sensitive details unnecessarily.
INHERITANCE-011
Forbidden Inheritance Behavior
Forbidden: hidden legacy revenue capture Forbidden: platform seizure of creator identity Forbidden: silent beneficiary changes Forbidden: erasing creator attribution Forbidden: unreviewed dispute continuation Forbidden: turning legacy into anonymous content inventory
INHERITANCE-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd inheritance routing exists to protect: human contribution creator dignity legacy revenue educational continuity and cultural memory. The larger cultural motion survives when human value remains attributed, honored, and responsibly routed.
