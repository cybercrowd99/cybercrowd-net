Archive bucket and canonical truth layer.

The Revelation

CyberCrowd was not accidentally compartmentalized. It was designed through survivable operational separation long before the doctrine language existed.

Truth, governance, continuity, sovereignty, resilience, intake, processing, canon, and public surface emerged as separate universes because the system was never intended to collapse from one failure.

CyberCrowd survives resets because no single compartment is the system.

The doctrine survives because the compartments survive independently.
