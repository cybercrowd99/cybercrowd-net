PixelPrix Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 8, 2026

PixelPrix is a CyberCade clean-up camera game. The game is designed so a room can become a simple local play activity.

What PixelPrix uses

PixelPrix may use the device camera and microphone when the player chooses to play the camera game.

  • Camera: used to take room, treasure, and before/after pictures for the game.
  • Microphone: used to record a short local voice replay after a treasure picture, so the player can hear their own word during cleanup.
  • Local storage: used to keep temporary game pictures, voice replay clips, and simple game settings on the device.

What stays local

PixelPrix is intended to keep pictures and voice replays on the device.

PixelPrix does not require an account, login, public profile, upload, cloud sync, ads, analytics, or social sharing for the local game flow.

What PixelPrix does not do

  • PixelPrix does not sell children’s data.
  • PixelPrix does not use the microphone to identify a person.
  • PixelPrix does not use speech-to-text for the treasure name flow.
  • PixelPrix does not publish pictures or voice clips.
  • PixelPrix does not run advertising or behavioral ad tracking in the local game flow.

Children and family use

PixelPrix can be used by families. A parent or guardian should supervise use of the camera and microphone. The game is built around local play and cleanup, not public posting.

Permissions

Camera and microphone permission are requested only when the game needs them. If permission is denied, touch play should still work where possible.

Clearing local game data

Players or parents can clear local game memory from the PixelPrix flow when the clear option is present. Clearing removes local temporary game pictures and local voice replay clips from that device.
