# GOVERNANCE_STABILITY_ANCHOR

## Purpose
The Governance Stability Anchor keeps the system steady during uncertainty, pressure, or rapid change.

## What It Maintains
- steady rules  
- clear direction  
- calm responses  
- consistent decisions  
- protection from sudden swings  

## How It Works
- holds core rules in place  
- slows down risky changes  
- keeps decisions predictable  
- shields the system from panic  
- maintains long-term direction  

## Why It Matters
Without stability, governance can break under pressure. The anchor keeps everything steady and safe.

## Summary
The stability anchor protects governance from chaos by keeping rules and direction firm during tough moments.
