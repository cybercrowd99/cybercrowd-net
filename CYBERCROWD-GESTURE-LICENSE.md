# CyberCrowd Gesture License

CyberCrowd may use body, head, click, toggle, and movement cues as access signals.

## Core Gesture Meanings

### X-Hard Break

An X gesture means STOP.

It is a hard break signal.

When detected, CyberCrowd must pause the action, cancel the pending move, or stop the current flow.

No purchase, vote, entry, broadcast, upload, payment, or authority action should continue after an X-Hard Break unless the user clearly restarts.

### X-Embrace With Head Bow

An X-embrace gesture with a head bow may count as YES only when the system has already asked a clear yes/no question.

This is not a blanket consent signal.

It only applies to the current prompt on screen.

### Three-Cue Purchase Rule

A purchase requires three separate YES cues.

A single gesture is not enough.

The three cues may be:

1. Visual confirmation on screen.
2. Physical yes gesture or head-bow confirmation.
3. Final click, toggle, or button confirmation.

If all three cues are not present, no purchase is completed.

## Movement Choices

For movement, selection, or navigation, the user may choose by:

- A / B movement
- toggle click
- button click
- head cue
- approved accessibility cue

## Consent Boundary

CyberCrowd gestures are access signals, not ownership transfer.

No gesture may be used to force consent, hide a purchase, skip a warning, or bypass a user’s right to stop.

## Safety Rule

STOP always outranks YES.

X-Hard Break always cancels the active action.
