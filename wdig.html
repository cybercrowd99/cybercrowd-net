WDIG – Wide Distributed Interactive Grid

CyberCrowd’s real‑time multi‑cam engine.

What is WDIG?

WDIG is the live backbone of CyberCrowd. It synchronizes phones, drones, VR rigs, 3D cams, and other devices into a single time‑aligned event grid. Viewers become signal contributors, and operators control the flow of the event.

How WDIG Works

Signal Contributors

Viewers attach cams voluntarily. Their feeds can be highlighted, mixed, or queued by the operator.

Operator Control

Operators choose which cams to show, mute, or drop. WDIG never overrides operator decisions except in safety‑critical cases.

Time Spine Sync

All feeds align to a shared time spine. Out‑of‑sync or corrupted feeds may be buffered or dropped to protect event integrity.

Safety Layers

WDIG includes automated checks for prohibited content, malicious feeds, and extreme instability.

Viewer Rights

Contributors may withdraw at any time. Operators may not use viewer cams for humiliation or harassment.
