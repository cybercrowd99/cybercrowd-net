# ECONOMY_RESERVE_TANK

## Purpose
The Economy Reserve Tank stores backup value for emergencies or sudden shortages. It keeps the system safe when supply drops or demand rises fast.

## What It Stores
- extra value  
- unused resources  
- emergency supply  
- backup flow  
- safety reserves  

## How It Works
- fills during calm times  
- releases during shortages  
- protects weak areas  
- prevents collapse  
- keeps flow steady  

## Why It Matters
A reserve tank makes the system stronger and safer. It helps the economy survive sudden changes without breaking.

## Summary
The reserve tank is the backup supply that keeps the economy stable during tough moments.
