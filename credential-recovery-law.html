CYBERCROWD CREDENTIAL RECOVERY LAW V1
Credential recovery doctrine for CyberCrowd continuity systems. This document defines how identities, credentials, connector authority, and operational trust are restored after: loss, compromise, failure, severance, or containment events.
RECOVERY-001
Core Rule
Recovery exists to restore continuity safely. Recovery must never: silently escalate authority silently bypass containment silently erase evidence silently rewrite continuity history Recovery restores trust through visible reconstruction.
RECOVERY-002
Recovery Classes
Recovery Class Purpose Approval Level
creator recovery restore creator continuity creator verification
connector recovery restore external platform access creator + replay review
sponsor recovery restore sponsor continuity contract review
governance recovery restore operational authority critical multi-review
sandbox recovery restore isolated test continuity sandbox-only
forensic recovery restore after incident review manual approval
RECOVERY-003
Identity Verification Rule
Recovery should verify: - continuity identity - creator identity - route ownership - connector ownership - recovery request chronology - replay continuity if required - trusted verification factors Verification must remain: replay-visible reviewable recoverable
RECOVERY-004
Replay Preservation Rule
Recovery must preserve: - alarm chronology - replay chronology - route chronology - migration chronology - containment chronology - revocation chronology Recovery must not erase: evidence containment history forensic lineage
RECOVERY-005
Recovery Escalation
Escalation may occur when: - identity confidence is low - replay divergence exists - route anomalies exist - authority scope is critical - forensic review remains unresolved - governance systems are affected Escalation increases: human review verification depth containment duration
RECOVERY-006
Manual Authority Rule
Critical recovery must remain human-governed. The system may assist: verification comparison chronology reconstruction alarm review The system may not: silently restore critical authority silently bypass human review
RECOVERY-007
Replacement Continuity Rule
If original continuity cannot safely recover: - replacement continuity may activate - replacement nodes may deploy - replacement routes may restore continuity while: evidence remains preserved damaged continuity remains reviewable
RECOVERY-008
Credential Rotation Rule
Recovery may require: - token rotation - connector rotation - route reassignment - trust downgrade - authority reduction - staged restoration Restoration should occur gradually when compromise risk exists.
RECOVERY-009
DECchamber Recovery Path
If continuity entered DECchamber: - forensic review occurs first - replay review occurs first - route analysis occurs first - containment verification occurs first Recovery may result in: restoration downgrade continued isolation permanent severance
RECOVERY-010
Forbidden Recovery Behavior
Forbidden: silent authority restoration Forbidden: evidence deletion during recovery Forbidden: hidden connector reactivation Forbidden: recovery without replay visibility Forbidden: cross-tier credential inheritance Forbidden: restoration without containment review
RECOVERY-011
Final Rule
CyberCrowd continuity survives compromise through governed reconstruction. Recovery is not: panic reset Recovery is: visible trust rebuilding through continuity preservation, review, containment, and human authority.
