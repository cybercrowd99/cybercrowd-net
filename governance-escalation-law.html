CYBERCROWD GOVERNANCE ESCALATION LAW V1
Governance escalation doctrine for CyberCrowd continuity systems. This document defines how operational authority escalates during: critical incidents, continuity destabilization, infrastructure compromise, and ecosystem-level emergencies while preserving replay visibility, human accountability, and continuity survivability.
GOV-001
Core Rule
Governance escalation exists to preserve continuity survivability. Escalation must: stabilize contain preserve evidence preserve continuity preserve accountability without silently centralizing unlimited authority.
GOV-002
Escalation Tiers
Tier Condition Operational Scope
TIER 1 localized instability compartment review
TIER 2 regional continuity degradation regional stabilization
TIER 3 major operational compromise multi-compartment control
TIER 4 ecosystem-level threat critical continuity governance
TIER 5 survival continuity state maximum containment preservation
GOV-003
Governance Authority Boundaries
Governance may: - freeze continuity compartments - isolate relay systems - revoke compromised authority - preserve replay continuity - activate emergency relays - activate continuity stabilization - approve restoration Governance may not: silently erase evidence silently rewrite replay silently conceal escalation
GOV-004
Emergency Operational States
Emergency operational states may include: - relay stabilization state - continuity freeze state - replay preservation state - route containment state - emergency migration state - governance visibility state - recovery staging state Emergency state transitions should remain: replay-visible reviewable recoverable
GOV-005
Escalation Trigger Conditions
Escalation may occur when: - forensic alarms activate - relay collapse risk appears - governance compromise appears - continuity fragmentation risk appears - coordinated disruption appears - replay instability appears - emergency route overload appears - large-scale migration instability appears
GOV-006
Governance Replay Visibility
Replay systems should preserve: - escalation chronology - governance activation chronology - compartment freeze chronology - relay stabilization chronology - restoration chronology - emergency routing chronology Governance continuity should remain observable.
GOV-007
Distributed Governance Continuity
Governance survivability should distribute through: - regional governance relays - emergency governance continuity - replacement governance compartments - replay-preserved governance reconstruction The ecosystem should avoid: single-governance dependency collapse.
GOV-008
Containment Rule
Escalation containment should target: affected continuity compartments Examples: - compromised relays - compromised connectors - compromised authority scopes - unstable replay systems - unstable migrations The larger continuity ecosystem should survive independently.
GOV-009
Restoration Authority Rule
Restoration after escalation requires: - replay review - forensic review - continuity review - relay review - recovery verification - human governance approval Containment may automate. Restoration remains human-governed.
GOV-010
Universal Stop Governance Rule
Universal stop command: "1" may activate: - continuity freeze - relay freeze - migration freeze - replay preservation - emergency stabilization - governance preservation state The stop command exists to preserve: continuity survivability.
GOV-011
Forbidden Governance Behavior
Forbidden: silent emergency authority expansion Forbidden: concealed escalation Forbidden: destroying replay evidence Forbidden: cross-tier authority abuse Forbidden: hidden governance routing Forbidden: permanent emergency authority without review
GOV-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd governance exists to preserve: continuity accountability visibility recovery and cultural survivability. The larger cultural motion survives through governed continuity escalation, not invisible centralized control.
