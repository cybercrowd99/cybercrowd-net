CYBERCROWD NODE TRUST ESCALATION LADDER V1
Operational trust doctrine for distributed CyberCrowd hardware. This matrix defines how devices gradually earn trust participation without automatically receiving authority escalation.
TRUST-001
Core Rule
Participation is not authority. A device may: participate observe relay heartbeat replay simulate without receiving: canon authority governance authority lineage authority archive authority Trust escalates slowly. Authority escalates carefully.

TRUST-003
Level 0 Rules
Unknown devices: - may not access replay - may not transport payloads - may not mutate routes - may not participate in governance Allowed: - initial registration - sandbox enrollment - heartbeat probation Default state: isolated restricted non-authoritative
TRUST-004
Level 1 Observer Trust
Observer trust allows: - public surface rendering - teaser playback - heartbeat continuity - limited replay observation Forbidden: - creator upload - unrestricted transport - governance participation - archive access - lineage mutation Typical use: old phones observer tablets view-only nodes
TRUST-005
Level 2 Probationary Continuity
Probationary nodes may: - participate in sandbox replay - simulate continuity - perform controlled transport tests - participate in isolated handoff testing Requirements: - stable heartbeat history - replay consistency - no quarantine triggers - verified sandbox isolation Probationary trust is still restricted.
TRUST-006
Level 3 Verified Continuity
Verified continuity allows: - controlled transport participation - replay continuity participation - creator continuity - relay continuity - controlled mesh participation Still forbidden: - governance override - archive mutation - canon rewrite - unrestricted escalation
TRUST-007
Level 4 Governance-Reviewed Trust
Governance-reviewed nodes require: - verified identity - replay continuity history - stable heartbeat continuity - audit history - controlled environment - review eligibility May: - approve release review - approve rollback review - freeze transport - quarantine suspicious nodes
TRUST-008
Level 5 Restricted Operational Authority
Highest-trust nodes may: - participate in governance operations - coordinate continuity restoration - coordinate rollback - coordinate replay reconstruction - coordinate operational freeze Still forbidden: silent canon rewrite silent replay destruction silent lineage destruction untracked governance mutation
TRUST-009

Escalation Requirements Review Needed

1 → 2 stable heartbeat + sandbox participation yes

4 → 5 restricted operational approval mandatory
TRUST-010
Quarantine Triggers
Automatic downgrade or quarantine: - replay corruption - heartbeat instability - unauthorized transport - lineage mutation attempts - governance spoofing - repeated deadletter failures - cache poisoning attempts - silent route mutation Quarantine route: SEQUESTER + GOVERNANCE review
TRUST-011
Downgrade + Rehabilitation
Trust is reversible. A device may be downgraded after: - instability - suspicious routing - failed replay continuity - governance violation Rehabilitation path: sandbox isolation + replay verification + heartbeat stabilization + review approval No permanent silent exile without review.
TRUST-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd survives distributed scale by separating: hardware participation from authority ownership. A cheap device may earn trust slowly. Trust is proven through continuity, not assumed through presence.
