Hierarchy Interpretation

Doctrine compartments exist above runtime implementation. Lanes move operational state horizontally. Buckets absorb timing, pressure, retries, and survivability load. Surface lanes expose approved canonical reality.

  • Doctrine governs lanes.
  • Compartments define responsibility.
  • Buckets preserve continuity.
  • Surface reads canon.
  • No lane silently mutates another lane.
Doctrine → Compartments → Lanes → Buckets → Canon → Surface
