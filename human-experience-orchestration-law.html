CYBERCROWD HUMAN EXPERIENCE ORCHESTRATION LAW V1
Human experience orchestration doctrine for CyberCrowd. This document defines how continuity environments may preserve: presence, rest, participation, atmosphere, learning, healing, and cultural immersion without relying on interruption-based engagement systems.
HUMAN-001
Core Rule
Humans are not engagement machines. CyberCrowd continuity should support: presence immersion recovery learning creation participation and atmosphere without requiring constant interruption.

HUMAN-003
Passive Participation Rule
Passive participation remains valid continuity. A human may: listen rest read study heal sleep or coexist within continuity environments without constant clicking, messaging, or reaction loops.
HUMAN-004
Sleep Continuity Rule
The user may intentionally authorize: uninterrupted passive continuity. Sleep continuity may support: - ambient audio - healing frequencies - rainfall continuity - calm music - night environments - recovery continuity - low-interruption environments Rest is also human continuity.
HUMAN-005
Atmosphere Continuity Rule
Continuity environments may support: - hotel lobbies - restaurants - waiting rooms - offices - healing spaces - study spaces - reading spaces - creative spaces - public ambience systems Atmosphere continuity preserves: human emotional environment.
HUMAN-006
Interruption Boundary Rule
The ecosystem should avoid: - aggressive interruption prompts - forced engagement loops - volume shock advertisements - panic engagement systems - rage bait systems - click bait dependency Continuity should remain: human-centered not interruption-centered.
HUMAN-007
Adworm Compatibility Rule
Adworm continuity should remain: ambient non-destructive continuity-aware and interruption-sensitive. Monetization should coexist with: human immersion not destroy it.
HUMAN-008
Accessibility-Oriented Orchestration
Human orchestration should support: - Deaf Companion systems - Slate systems - caption continuity - sign/speech bridge continuity - blind-mode continuity - haptic continuity - readable interfaces - low-stimulation environments Accessibility is continuity participation.
HUMAN-009
Continuity Pacing Rule
Not all continuity requires: speed urgency or constant stimulation. The ecosystem may support: slow continuity ambient continuity reflective continuity educational continuity and restorative continuity. The larger cultural motion survives even while the human rests.
HUMAN-010
Anti-Clickbait Principle
CyberCrowd moves away from: click bait attention weaponization rage loops and addictive interruption systems. Presence itself may hold value.
HUMAN-011
Forbidden Experience Behavior
Forbidden: weaponized interruption Forbidden: false urgency manipulation Forbidden: sleep-disruptive monetization Forbidden: passive continuity invalidation Forbidden: accessibility exclusion by experience design Forbidden: treating humans as engagement inventory
HUMAN-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd exists to preserve: human presence human continuity human atmosphere human participation and human recovery. The ecosystem remains alive through continuity-aware orchestration, not interruption addiction systems. The larger cultural motion survives.
