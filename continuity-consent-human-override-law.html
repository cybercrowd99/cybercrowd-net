CYBERCROWD CONTINUITY CONSENT HUMAN OVERRIDE LAW V1
Consent and human sovereignty doctrine for CyberCrowd continuity systems. This document defines how humans remain in control of continuity, transport, migration, replay, and distributed participation.
CONSENT-001
Core Rule
Humans remain the authority. Continuity exists to assist participation, not silently override human control. No device, transport layer, migration system, or continuity engine may silently seize operational authority.
CONSENT-002
Explicit Consent Requirement
The following actions require visible human consent: - node pairing - session migration - transport activation - clipboard transfer - drag transfer - remote cursor continuity - replay participation - creator upload continuity - VR continuity entry - accessibility continuity linkage No hidden activation.
CONSENT-003
Transport Approval Rule
Before transport begins, the user must see: - source device - target device - transport scope - trust level - replay visibility - heartbeat visibility - allowed authority - sandbox or Production status Transport begins only after confirmation.
CONSENT-004
Device Takeover Prevention
Forbidden: - silent cursor seizure - invisible clipboard access - hidden drag transfer - hidden session migration - forced replay enrollment - silent authority escalation - automatic device takeover Every continuity transition must remain: visible reviewable interruptible replay-visible
CONSENT-005
Revoke Continuity Rule
A user may revoke continuity at any time. Revocation may: - freeze transport - stop replay participation - stop heartbeat participation - disconnect node pairing - revoke session authority - trigger migration freeze - trigger sandbox downgrade Revocation must remain immediate and visible.
CONSENT-006
Emergency Disconnect Rule
Emergency disconnect immediately: - freezes transport - freezes migration - freezes cursor continuity - freezes clipboard continuity - freezes remote control continuity Emergency disconnect preserves: - replay evidence - heartbeat evidence - anchor continuity - lineage continuity Emergency disconnect is preservation, not destruction.
CONSENT-007
Session Freeze Rule
Users may freeze a session without terminating continuity. Freeze state preserves: - replay continuity - cursor continuity - migration state - authority scope - heartbeat evidence Frozen continuity may later: resume recover migrate or terminate safely.
CONSENT-008
Observer Consent Rule
Observer continuity requires: - visible participation - visible pairing - visible replay enrollment - visible heartbeat enrollment Observers must not become: hidden transport relays hidden governance nodes hidden archive participants
CONSENT-009
Creator Consent Rule
Creator continuity requires explicit approval for: - uploads - migration - replay continuity - cross-device continuity - collaborative continuity - archive continuity Creator continuity must preserve: ownership lineage authority boundaries
CONSENT-010
Accessibility Consent Rule
Accessibility systems require explicit and understandable consent. Examples: - Deaf Companion continuity - Slate continuity - speech bridge continuity - sign-language continuity - haptic continuity - voice relay continuity Accessibility continuity must remain: human-first transparent interruptible non-exploitative
CONSENT-011
Override Hierarchy
Priority Authority Can Override
1 human emergency stop everything
2 human revoke continuity transport + migration
3 governance freeze restricted operational systems
4 session freeze active continuity only
5 transport continuity cursor/clipboard continuity only
CONSENT-012
Universal Stop Rule
Universal stop command: "1" Meaning: - freeze continuity - preserve replay - preserve heartbeat - preserve anchors - preserve governance continuity - stop active transport - stop migration escalation The stop command exists for preservation and recovery.
CONSENT-013
Human-First Authority Rule
CyberCrowd continuity systems exist to extend human participation, not replace human sovereignty. Humans remain: the consent authority the transport authority the continuity authority the override authority The system assists. The human decides.
