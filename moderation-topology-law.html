CYBERCROWD MODERATION TOPOLOGY LAW V1
Moderation continuity doctrine for CyberCrowd. This document defines how moderation systems: observe, contain, escalate, preserve evidence, and protect continuity without collapsing the ecosystem into hidden authority abuse.
MOD-001
Core Rule
Moderation exists to preserve healthy continuity. Moderation should: protect participants protect continuity preserve evidence preserve visibility contain abuse without silently rewriting reality.
MOD-002
Moderation Compartments
Moderation Compartment Purpose Authority Scope
community moderation routine participation protection limited
creator moderation creator-owned continuity spaces creator scoped
relay moderation relay continuity protection regional/system
incident moderation active continuity threat handling high
governance moderation critical operational continuity critical
sandbox moderation non-production experimentation isolated
MOD-003
Moderation Replay Visibility
Moderation actions should become replay-visible. Replay may preserve: - moderation chronology - escalation chronology - isolation chronology - route freeze chronology - restoration chronology - warning chronology Replay should preserve: continuity truth not hidden authority.
MOD-004
Moderation Escalation
Escalation may occur when: - repeated abuse occurs - continuity destabilization occurs - verified threat behavior appears - coordinated disruption appears - governance continuity becomes threatened - forensic alarms activate Escalation increases: visibility containment human review forensic preservation
MOD-005
Isolation Rule
Moderation may isolate: - routes - audience compartments - creator compartments - relay lanes - connectors - replay surfaces - migration paths Isolation should target: affected continuity compartments not destroy unrelated continuity.
MOD-006
Moderation Relay Continuity
Moderation systems may distribute through: - regional moderation relays - emergency moderation relays - incident continuity relays - governance continuity relays Distributed moderation supports: continuity survivability.
MOD-007
Authority Scope Rule
Moderation authority remains compartmentalized. A moderator must not: silently escalate into governance authority silently rewrite replay silently erase evidence silently bypass containment law Moderation is operational protection, not invisible ownership.
MOD-008
Moderation Recovery Rule
Recovery after moderation action may require: - replay review - continuity review - route review - creator review - governance review if escalated Recovery should preserve: evidence chronology continuity lineage
MOD-009
Forensic Preservation Rule
Moderation incidents should preserve: - replay chronology - route chronology - connector chronology - relay chronology - alarm chronology - moderation chronology Moderation should not destroy: forensic continuity evidence.
MOD-010
Audience Protection Rule
Moderation should prioritize: - audience safety - creator continuity safety - accessibility continuity - emergency continuity visibility - transparency The larger cultural motion survives through healthy continuity protection.
MOD-011
Forbidden Moderation Behavior
Forbidden: hidden moderation manipulation Forbidden: silent replay destruction Forbidden: cross-tier authority abuse Forbidden: silent creator suppression Forbidden: concealed moderation escalation Forbidden: erasing continuity evidence
MOD-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd moderation exists to: protect continuity protect participants preserve truth and preserve cultural motion. Moderation should remain: visible reviewable recoverable and operationally accountable.
