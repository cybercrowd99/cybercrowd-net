CYBERCROWD ARTIFICIAL OPERATIONAL SANDBOXES V1
Artificial operational sandbox doctrine for CyberCrowd. This document defines isolated experimentation environments that protect live continuity systems from simulated traffic.
SANDBOX-001
Core Rule
Fake systems test behavior. Real systems preserve truth. Simulation must never silently contaminate: - replay continuity - lineage continuity - governance continuity - archive continuity - canonical continuity Artificial operational sandboxes exist to isolate experimentation from living operational truth.
SANDBOX-002

Bucket Purpose Allowed Content
DEV_REPLAY fake continuity testing simulated replay chains
DEV_HEARTBEATS heartbeat simulation fake node pulses
DEV_SEQUESTER intake experimentation fake payloads
DEV_DEADLETTER failure injection simulated failures
SANDBOX-003
STAGING Sandbox Buckets
Bucket Purpose Allowed Content
STAGING_REPLAY controlled replay validation near-production replay simulation
STAGING_HEARTBEATS continuity validation controlled heartbeat tests
STAGING_SEQUESTER validation proofing controlled intake tests
STAGING_DEADLETTER rollback-safe failures controlled failure scenarios
SANDBOX-004
Forbidden Sandbox Behavior
Forbidden: DEV → Production replay lane Forbidden: STAGING → Production canon writes Forbidden: fake events → LEDGER_EVENTS Forbidden: sandbox heartbeat → live heartbeat lane Forbidden: simulated uploads → ARCHIVE Forbidden: test governance → live governance Forbidden: sandbox routes bypassing SEQUESTER
SANDBOX-005
Why No DEV_LEDGER_EVENTS
No DEV_LEDGER_EVENTS bucket exists. Reason: canon truth should never become fake truth. Replay may simulate continuity. Heartbeat may simulate liveness. Transport may simulate movement. But canon lanes preserve operational truth.
SANDBOX-006
Sandbox Prefix Rule
All sandbox objects must use visible prefixes: dev/ staging/ mock/ simulation/ failure-test/ Forbidden: hidden fake objects in live operational paths.
SANDBOX-007
Sandbox Replay Rule
Sandbox replay systems may: - reconstruct fake sessions - reconstruct fake handoffs - simulate continuity recovery - simulate interruption Sandbox replay systems may not: - mutate Production replay - overwrite canon - inject lineage history
SANDBOX-008
Sandbox Transport Rule
Sandbox transport may test: - fake cursor movement - fake clipboard events - fake drag events - fake handoff continuity - fake latency - fake disconnects Sandbox transport must remain: isolated replayable containable non-public
SANDBOX-009
Promotion Rule
Feature promotion sequence: DEV → STAGING → controlled proof → governance review → Production release No direct DEV → Production jump.
SANDBOX-010
Cloudflare Discipline Rule
Sandbox bucket creation must follow: <[team build]> Meaning: - one TASK only - explicit containment - full active binding restatement - no broad auto-write authority - verify before next mutation
SANDBOX-011
Future Sandbox Expansion
Future artificial sandbox systems: - DEV_CURSOR_TRANSPORT - DEV_VR_CONTINUITY - DEV_OBSERVER_SWARM - STAGING_CURSOR_TRANSPORT - STAGING_MESH_ROUTER - STAGING_REPLAY_RECOVERY Expansion remains compartmentalized.
SANDBOX-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd experiments safely by building artificial operational worlds separate from living continuity. Sandbox systems exist so the real platform can survive growth.
