PixelPrix Family Safety

PixelPrix is built as a local clean-up camera game.

Parent / guardian note

PixelPrix may use the camera and microphone during play. The intended use is simple:

  • take a room picture,
  • take treasure pictures,
  • record a short voice replay for each treasure,
  • play that voice replay back during cleanup.

Voice replay rule

The point is not speech-to-text. The point is that the child hears their own recorded word come back during cleanup.

Local-only design

The PixelPrix local game flow is designed without accounts, uploads, public posts, ads, analytics, or social sharing.

Safe use

  • Use PixelPrix in a safe room.
  • Do not photograph private documents, addresses, passwords, payment cards, or sensitive items.
  • Parents or guardians should supervise children using camera or microphone features.
