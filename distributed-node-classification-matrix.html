CYBERCROWD DISTRIBUTED NODE CLASSIFICATION MATRIX V1
Distributed node authority doctrine for CyberCrowd. This matrix classifies operational hardware surfaces by authority level, replay eligibility, transport eligibility, and continuity trust.
NODE-001
Core Rule
Hardware is not authority. Devices participate through: - verified identity - verified session - verified replay continuity - verified heartbeat continuity - governance-approved scope Cheap hardware may participate safely when authority separation exists.

NODE-003
Observer Node Rules
Observer nodes may: - receive public surfaces - receive teaser playback - emit heartbeat continuity - participate in replay continuity Observer nodes may not: - mutate canon - mutate lineage - mutate governance - bypass SEQUESTER - perform unrestricted transport
NODE-004
Sandbox Node Rules
Sandbox nodes exist for: - fake transport testing - replay simulation - heartbeat simulation - handoff testing - continuity failure testing Sandbox nodes must remain: isolated non-authoritative non-canonical non-production Factory-reset devices are ideal sandbox nodes.
NODE-005
Creator Node Rules
Creator nodes may: - upload content - route through SEQUESTER - establish lineage - participate in replay continuity - initiate transport continuity Creator nodes may not: - bypass intake law - bypass replay registration - bypass governance review - self-assign authority escalation
NODE-006
Relay Node Rules
Relay nodes may: - preserve continuity - relay heartbeat traffic - maintain replay continuity - stabilize mesh transport Relay nodes may not: - become canon authority - mutate lineage - silently cache governance records - self-promote authority
NODE-007
Governance Node Rules
Governance nodes require: - secured environment - multi-factor verification - replay registration - audit continuity - restricted transport Governance nodes may: - freeze routes - freeze transport - quarantine nodes - trigger rollback review Governance nodes may not: - silently rewrite canon - erase replay history - bypass audit continuity

NODE-009
Transport Eligibility
Transport eligibility requires: - verified session - replay continuity - heartbeat continuity - authority scope - governance-safe routing Low-trust hardware may participate in transport only through restricted continuity lanes.
NODE-010
Old Hardware Doctrine
Old phones and old laptops are resources. When factory-reset and sandbox-isolated they become: - continuity nodes - replay nodes - observer nodes - transport test nodes - relay nodes - telemetry surfaces CyberCrowd converts discarded hardware into distributed operational infrastructure.
NODE-011
Forbidden Node Behavior
Forbidden: sandbox node → Production canon authority Forbidden: observer node → governance authority Forbidden: relay node → lineage mutation Forbidden: low-trust hardware → unrestricted transport Forbidden: anonymous node → archive authority
NODE-012
Final Rule
CyberCrowd survives scale by separating hardware participation from authority ownership. A cheap device may join the network safely when continuity, containment, replay, and authority law exist first.
