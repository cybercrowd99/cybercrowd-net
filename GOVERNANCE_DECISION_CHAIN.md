# GOVERNANCE_DECISION_CHAIN

## Purpose
The Governance Decision Chain explains how decisions move from request to action. It keeps the system organized and prevents confusion.

## What It Tracks
- where a request starts  
- who reviews it  
- how approval works  
- how action is triggered  
- how results are confirmed  

## Why It Matters
A clear decision chain keeps the system from making mistakes or acting out of order.

## Summary
The decision chain is the path every choice follows to keep the system steady and predictable.
