PERMISSION PHILOSOPHY

Cybercrowd permissions are not designed to trap users behind walls.

Permissions exist to:
• preserve trust
• protect environments
• prevent chaos
• stage progression naturally
• allow growth through interaction ownership

The environment remains explorable. Ownership evolves with trust.
