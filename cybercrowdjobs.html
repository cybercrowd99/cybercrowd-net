CyberCrowdJobs

CyberCrowdJobs is the workforce leg attached to CyberCrowd.net. This is not a generic job board. This page is the operating lobby for live worker résumés, clock-in visibility, client pings, service clearance, trust records, CyberCrowd Services routing, Salvage support, and HDF route evidence.

The résumé is not uploaded. The résumé is earned.

Leg CyberCrowdJobs
Parent CyberCrowd.net
Method Résumé → Ping → Job
Status Foundation Lobby

Worker Clock-In

A worker becomes visible when they clock in for an approved lane, route, service type, or availability window.

Live Résumé

The live résumé is generated by deeds: completed jobs, camera proof, rates, boundaries, guarantees, repair windows, refund rules, availability, and trust pings.

Client Ping

The client reviews the record first. If the client wants the worker, the client sends a ping. No bidding. No begging. No sales theater.

Service Clearance

The system may check skill match, route match, known-connection risk, privacy limits, customer approval, and trust status before work starts.

CyberCrowd Services

CyberCrowd Services attaches to CyberCrowdJobs for real-world service execution. The service mission is simple: We Deliver Time.

Salvage Lane

CyberCrowd Services / Salvage can use QR custody, HDF events, separation pings, route evidence, and Market Connect logic for recovery and asset movement.

Operating Flow

Worker clocks in.

The worker’s live résumé becomes visible only inside the approved lane, route, service category, and permission boundary.

CyberCrowdJobs opens the live résumé.

The system shows verified job history, camera proof, rates, boundaries, guarantees, availability, trust pings, and service restrictions.

Client reviews and sends a ping.

The client does not receive a sales pitch. The client reviews the deed record and sends a ping if the worker fits the need.

Clearance checks run.

CyberCrowdJobs may check identity, session trust, service fit, route fit, known-connection rules, privacy firewalls, and customer approval before the job is accepted.

Job moves into service routing.

Approved work can route toward CyberCrowd Services, future Salvage lanes, HDF evidence, dashboard visibility, and ledger records.

Foundation Status

This page creates the CyberCrowdJobs operating lobby. It does not claim the full job engine, payout engine, worker onboarding engine, HDF routing engine, or service dispatch system is complete.

The purpose is to attach the Jobs leg to CyberCrowd.net so routing, profiles, dashboards, pings, services, and future Salvage lanes have a visible foundation to connect to.

Boundary Rule

CyberCrowdJobs is not a detached job board. It is a workforce leg of CyberCrowd.net. It must connect cleanly, disconnect cleanly, and route through the filing-cabinet system without scattering the platform.
