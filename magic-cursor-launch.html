MAGIC CURSOR V5
Persistent Shared Sandbox + Transferable Interaction Ownership

Launch Instructions

STEP 1

Open terminal or command line in your Cybercrowd project folder.
STEP 2

Start the sandbox server:
node server.js
STEP 3

Laptop launch path:
http://localhost:7070
STEP 4

Phone launch path:
http://YOUR-LAPTOP-IP:7070/?client
STEP 5

Push cursor to right edge of laptop screen to transfer interaction ownership to phone.
STEP 6

Touch left edge on phone to return interaction ownership to laptop.

Sandbox Logic

Traditional systems:

open app → close app → lose state

Cybercrowd V5:

persistent shared sandbox + transferable interaction ownership

The environment survives. Only interaction ownership changes.

Objects remain persistent between surfaces. Laptop and phone become collaborative environment layers.
